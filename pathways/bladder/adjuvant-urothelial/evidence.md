# Adjuvant urothelial — clinic evidence brief

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14
Owner: GU clinic
Purpose: 60-second pre-visit skim (not a full guideline)
Status: stub — expand from NCCN / landmark papers on next refresh

## Bottom line (clinic)
- High-risk resected urothelial carcinoma may warrant adjuvant IO (e.g. nivolumab per CheckMate 274 criteria) vs observation; perioperative EV+pembrolizumab is reshaping MIBC — confirm current perioperative pathway before counseling.

## Who this applies to
- Post-cystectomy (or appropriately selected post-nephroureterectomy) with high-risk pathologic features; note neoadjuvant exposure.

## Standard options (decision tree sketch)
- Observation vs adjuvant IO vs clinical trial; align with neoadjuvant regimen already given.

## Landmark evidence (names + what they established)
- CheckMate 274; evolving perioperative EV+pembro / NIAGARA-class data — re-check before clinic.

## Biomarkers / testing that change therapy
- PD-L1 not always required for adjuvant nivolumab decision — confirm current label/NCCN.

## Toxicity / counseling anchors
- IO irAEs; surgical recovery constraints.

## Watch / pending practice-changers
- Perioperative ADC+IO becoming SOC for many MIBC patients.
