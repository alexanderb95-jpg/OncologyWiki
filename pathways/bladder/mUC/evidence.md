# Metastatic urothelial carcinoma — clinic evidence brief

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14
Owner: GU clinic
Purpose: 60-second pre-visit skim (not a full guideline)
Status: stub — expand from NCCN / landmark papers on next refresh

## Bottom line (clinic)
- First-line for many: enfortumab vedotin + pembrolizumab; alternatives depend on cisplatin eligibility, FGFR, prior therapy, and trial options.

## Who this applies to
- Unresectable locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma.

## Standard options (decision tree sketch)
- EV+pembro; platinum chemo; maintenance avelumab (post-platinum); EV or SG; erdafitinib if FGFR; IO monotherapy in selected settings.

## Landmark evidence (names + what they established)
- EV-302; JAVELIN Bladder 100; KEYNOTE/IMVigor lineages; THOR (FGFR) — re-verify.

## Biomarkers / testing that change therapy
- FGFR2/3; HER2 (emerging); PD-L1 as relevant to regimen.

## Toxicity / counseling anchors
- EV neuropathy/rash/hyperglycemia; IO irAEs; platinum toxicities.

## Watch / pending practice-changers
- HER2 ADCs; sequencing after EV+pembro.
