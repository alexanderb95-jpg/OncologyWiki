# mHSPC — clinic evidence brief

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14
Owner: GU clinic
Purpose: 60-second pre-visit skim (not a full guideline)
Status: living stub — re-check numbers against NCCN / primary papers on refresh

## Bottom line (clinic)
- Newly diagnosed metastatic HSPC is treated with intensified systemic therapy, not ADT alone, for most fit patients.
- Backbone: ADT + ARPI (e.g. darolutamide, enzalutamide, apalutamide, or abiraterone/prednisone per indication and comorbidity).
- Triplet (ADT + ARPI + docetaxel) is considered mainly for high-volume / high-risk disease in fit patients; incremental benefit of chemo on top of modern ADT+ARPI is setting- and trial-specific — counsel uncertainty where relevant.
- Up-front molecular testing (germline + somatic) and PSMA imaging inform later Pluvicto / PARP options and counseling even when not used day 1.
- Supportive care from day 1: bone health (Ca/Vit D, DEXA, denosumab/zoledronic when indicated), CV risk (statin discussion), sexual/QoL side effects of ADT.

## Who this applies to
- Synchronous or metachronous metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC / mCSPC).
- Subclassify: high vs low volume (CHAARTED-style), de novo vs recurrent, visceral disease, fitness for docetaxel, comorbidities (hepatic, cardiac, falls).

## Standard options (decision tree sketch)
- Fit, most patients: ADT + ARPI doublet.
- Fit, high-volume / high-risk: discuss ADT + ARPI ± docetaxel (triplet).
- PSMA+ / later lines: Pluvicto enters counseling for progressive disease after ARPI (± prior taxane per label/indication evolution) — not usually first systemic step in classic mHSPC.
- HRR-deficient (esp. BRCA): PARP (± ARPI combinations per approved setting) more relevant at CRPC, but test early.
- Local therapy: prostate RT / metastasis-directed therapy in selected low-volume / oligometastatic cases — coordinate with rad onc / urology.

## Landmark evidence (names + what they established)
- CHAARTED / STAMPEDE (docetaxel arms) — chemo intensification in mHSPC.
- LATITUDE / STAMPEDE (abi) — abi + ADT.
- ARCHES / ENZAMET — enzalutamide + ADT.
- TITAN — apalutamide + ADT.
- ARASENS — darolutamide + ADT + docetaxel triplet.
- PEACE-1 — abi + ADT ± docetaxel (± RT).
- Re-check current NCCN Prostate Cancer for sequencing, volume definitions, and any new up-front radioligand / PARP data before clinic.

## Biomarkers / testing that change therapy
- Germline (e.g. Invitae panel) + somatic NGS (blood/tissue) for HRR / BRCA / MSI-H.
- PSMA PET for staging and future RLT eligibility.
- Testosterone to confirm castration once on ADT; PSA kinetics for response.

## Toxicity / counseling anchors
- ADT: fatigue, hot flashes, weight gain, sarcopenia, ED/libido, mood, CV risk, bone loss.
- ARPI class: fatigue; drug-specific — darolutamide (cytopenias, hepatotoxicity, cardiac); others per agent.
- Docetaxel: neuropathy, cytopenias, infection risk, alopecia, fatigue.

## Watch / pending practice-changers
- Up-front or early Pluvicto combinations in PSMA+ HSPC.
- PARP combinations moving earlier in HRR+ disease.
- Metastasis-directed therapy and prostate RT in oligometastatic HSPC.
