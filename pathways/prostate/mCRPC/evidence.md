Purpose: 60-second pre-visit skim (not a full guideline)

## Bottom line (clinic)
- Castration-resistant metastatic disease after ADT ± prior ARPI/chemo.
- Sequencing is prior-therapy and biomarker dependent (ARPI, taxane, Pluvicto if PSMA+, PARP if HRR+, Ra-223 if bone-predominant).

## Who this applies to
- Progressive disease on ADT with castrate testosterone; document prior ARPI/chemo/RLT.

## Standard options (decision tree sketch)
- Choose next line by prior exposure, PSMA status, HRR/BRCA, symptoms, visceral disease, and trial availability.

## Landmark evidence (names + what they established)
- AFFIRM/PREVAIL, COU-AA-301/302, TAX327, CARD, VISION/PSMAfore (PSMA RLT), PROfound/PROpel/MAGNITUDE (PARP), ALSYMPCA (Ra-223) — re-verify current indications.

## Biomarkers / testing that change therapy
- PSMA PET; germline + somatic HRR; MSI/TMB when relevant.

## Toxicity / counseling anchors
- Match planned agent; bone health continues.

## Watch / pending practice-changers
- Earlier RLT; dual-targeted combinations; novel AR degraders.
