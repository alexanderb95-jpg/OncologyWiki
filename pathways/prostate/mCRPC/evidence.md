# mCRPC — clinic evidence brief

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14
Owner: GU clinic
Purpose: 60-second pre-visit skim (not a full guideline)
Status: living brief — re-check numbers against NCCN / primary papers on refresh

## Bottom line (clinic)
- Progressive metastatic disease with castrate testosterone; continue ADT indefinitely.
- Next line is driven by prior HSPC/CRPC therapy, PSMA PET, HRR/BRCA, symptoms, visceral disease, and fitness — there is no single best sequence.
- After an ARPI, a second ARPI is generally not the preferred switch. Discuss taxane (docetaxel if chemo-naive; cabazitaxel after prior docetaxel), Pluvicto if PSMA+, and PARP if BRCA / selected HRR.
- Test early (germline + somatic NGS, MSI/TMB) and obtain PSMA PET when radioligand therapy is on the table.
- Bone-protective agent (denosumab or zoledronic acid) for bone metastases; Ca/Vit D and dental clearance. Involve palliative care early when symptoms or later-line disease.

## Who this applies to
- mCRPC: radiographic and/or biochemical progression on ADT with castrate testosterone.
- Document de novo vs recurrent HSPC, volume, visceral disease, and the full prior-therapy list (ARPI, docetaxel in HSPC vs CRPC, RLT, PARP).
- Fitness for taxane, marrow reserve (for RLT or chemo), renal function, dental status, and whether visceral or bulky nodal disease excludes Ra-223.

## Standard options (decision tree sketch)
- Continue ADT in all patients.
- ARPI-naive: abiraterone/prednisone or enzalutamide. If BRCA (and selected HRR per label) and ARPI-naive: PARP + ARPI combination.
- After prior ARPI: if PSMA+, Pluvicto is an option including patients appropriate to delay taxane, or after prior taxane. Fit chemo-naive: docetaxel remains a standard alternative, especially if visceral/symptomatic or PSMA-negative/mixed. After ARPI + docetaxel: Pluvicto (PSMA+) or cabazitaxel.
- BRCA / selected HRR after ARPI: PARP monotherapy (olaparib for HRR per companion diagnostic; rucaparib for BRCA). Benefit is strongest in BRCA; non-BRCA HRR genes are gene-specific — do not treat all HRR hits as equivalent.
- Symptomatic bone-predominant, no visceral disease, no bulky nodes: radium-223 (do not pair with abiraterone).
- MSI-H/dMMR: pembrolizumab (later-line, histology-agnostic). Sipuleucel-T is optional in selected indolent, minimally symptomatic, non-visceral disease (no expected PSA/radiographic response).
- Clinical trial and best supportive care at every decision point.

## Landmark evidence (names + what they established)
- TAX327 — docetaxel OS benefit in mCRPC.
- TROPIC — cabazitaxel after docetaxel.
- CARD — cabazitaxel preferred over ARPI switch after docetaxel + ARPI.
- COU-AA-301 / 302 — abiraterone post- and pre-chemo.
- AFFIRM / PREVAIL — enzalutamide post- and pre-chemo.
- ALSYMPCA — radium-223 in symptomatic bone-predominant, non-visceral disease.
- VISION — Lu-PSMA-617 after ARPI and taxane in PSMA+ mCRPC.
- PSMAfore — Lu-PSMA-617 after one ARPI in taxane-naive patients considered appropriate to delay chemo (FDA expansion; rPFS benefit vs ARPI change; OS interpretation limited by crossover).
- PROfound — olaparib vs ARPI switch after ARPI in HRR; benefit concentrated in BRCA.
- TRITON2 / TRITON3 — rucaparib in BRCA mCRPC after ARPI.
- PROpel / MAGNITUDE / TALAPRO-2 — PARP + ARPI combinations; US labels are biomarker-restricted (BRCA for olaparib+abi and niraparib+abi; HRR gene list for talazoparib+enza) — check current FDA indication, not trial all-comer analyses.
- IMPACT — sipuleucel-T OS in selected minimally symptomatic disease.
- Re-check current NCCN Prostate Cancer, ASCO mCRPC guideline, and AUA advanced prostate cancer algorithm for sequencing before clinic.

## Biomarkers / testing that change therapy
- PSMA PET (approved tracer) for Pluvicto eligibility; mixed/PSMA-negative lesions argue against RLT.
- Germline + somatic NGS (tissue and/or ctDNA) for BRCA1/2 and other HRR genes; use the companion diagnostic that matches the intended PARP.
- MSI / dMMR / TMB for pembrolizumab (TMB-high benefit in prostate is modest — other active options first).
- Testosterone to confirm castration; PSA and conventional imaging for response (sipuleucel-T is an exception).

## Toxicity / counseling anchors
- Taxanes: neuropathy, cytopenias, infection, alopecia, fatigue; cabazitaxel — diarrhea; premeds / growth-factor per regimen.
- Pluvicto: xerostomia, myelosuppression, fatigue, nausea, renal; radiation precautions; marrow reserve and prior RLT/Ra-223 matter.
- PARP: cytopenias, nausea, fatigue, rare MDS/AML; drug–drug interactions with some ARPIs (talazoparib dose is combination-specific).
- ARPI: fatigue; abi — mineralocorticoid / hepatic / cardiac; enza — falls, CNS, seizure risk.
- Ra-223: myelosuppression, GI; do not combine with abi.
- Bone agents: hypocalcemia, ONJ, atypical fracture — dental eval, Ca/Vit D.

## Watch / pending practice-changers
- Earlier Pluvicto (PSMAddition already moved RLT into PSMA+ HSPC — prior HSPC RLT changes CRPC marrow/eligibility counseling).
- Other PSMA radioligands and combinations; AR degraders / novel AR ligands.
- Lineage plasticity / NEPC when PSA is low relative to disease burden — different playbook.
- Optimal sequence among Pluvicto, taxane, and PARP after ARPI remains unsettled.
