# Metastatic RCC — clinic evidence brief

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14
Owner: GU clinic
Purpose: 60-second pre-visit skim (not a full guideline)
Status: stub — expand from NCCN / landmark papers on next refresh

## Bottom line (clinic)
- Clear-cell mRCC first-line is usually IO/IO or IO/TKI by IMDC risk and comorbidity; non-clear-cell is histology-specific.

## Who this applies to
- Metastatic RCC; record histology and IMDC risk.

## Standard options (decision tree sketch)
- Dual IO (e.g. ipi/nivo) vs IO+TKI (pembrolizumab/axitinib, nivo/cabo, pembro/lenvatinib, etc.) per risk and goals.

## Landmark evidence (names + what they established)
- CheckMate 214; KEYNOTE-426; CheckMate 9ER; CLEAR — re-verify.

## Biomarkers / testing that change therapy
- Histology; occasional germline (VHL etc.); PD-L1 not primary selector for most doublets.

## Toxicity / counseling anchors
- irAEs; TKI class effects (HTN, hand-foot, diarrhea, hepatic).

## Watch / pending practice-changers
- Adjuvant pembrolizumab implications for relapsed patients; HIF-2 inhibitors.
