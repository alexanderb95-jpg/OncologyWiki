- Counseling: Surveillance is preferred when follow-up is reliable. One carboplatin cycle (TE19): 5-year relapse-free rate about 95% vs 96% with radiotherapy—not versus surveillance; does not prove survival benefit. One BEP vs RPLND (AH 01/94): 2-year recurrence-free survival about 99% vs 92%—does not compare either to surveillance. TRISST: MRI surveillance low advanced-relapse rates with ≥98% 5-year OS in all arms.
- Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: EAU minimal follow-up—seminoma CS I: markers about twice yearly years 1–3 then yearly years 4–5, abdominopelvic MRI/CT twice yearly years 1–2 then at 36 and 60 months (no routine chest X-ray); NSGCT on surveillance: markers about 4×/year years 1–2 (some use 6× if LVI+), chest X-ray twice yearly years 1–2 (± later if LVI+), abdominopelvic MRI/CT twice in year 1, at 24 months (±18 months if LVI+), then at 36 and 60 months. One adjuvant carboplatin or BEP cycle completes treatment without maintenance; marker rise or imaging relapse → metastatic GCT pathway at a germ-cell center.
- Counseling: Reviewed sequence: after orchiectomy, surveillance when feasible; one carboplatin cycle then surveillance for seminoma selecting relapse reduction; one BEP cycle then surveillance for higher-risk NSGCT; or selected nerve-sparing RPLND then structured surveillance. ***
- Monitoring: With carboplatin or BEP, check CBC/renal function and review fever, bleeding, nausea/vomiting, hearing, neuropathy, and pulmonary symptoms; after RPLND, review wound, urinary, sexual/ejaculatory, fertility, and thrombotic symptoms. ***
