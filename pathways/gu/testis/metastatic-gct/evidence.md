# Testis → Metastatic germ-cell tumor

Last reviewed: 2026-09-21
Next review: 2026-12-20
Owner: GU clinic
Purpose: Decision support for stage IIC/III or systemic germ-cell tumor requiring first-line chemotherapy
Status: current — treatment is IGCCCG-directed and should be coordinated by an experienced germ-cell tumor team

## Who this applies to

| Population | Definition | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Metastatic seminoma or NSGCT | Use prechemotherapy AFP, hCG, LDH, primary site, and nonpulmonary visceral metastases for IGCCCG classification. | Do not use pre-orchiectomy markers alone. |
| Good-risk NSGCT | Testis/retroperitoneal primary, no nonpulmonary visceral metastases, and AFP/hCG/LDH below IGCCCG intermediate thresholds. | Stage IIA/B marker-negative low-volume disease follows a separate RPLND/radiotherapy/chemotherapy decision pathway. |
| Intermediate-/poor-risk NSGCT or intermediate-risk seminoma | Match exact IGCCCG group and organ burden. | No poor-prognosis IGCCCG seminoma category exists. |

## Standard options

| Clinical context | Option | Key inclusion | Typical course |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good-risk seminoma or NSGCT | BEP ×3 | Cisplatin-based first-line treatment. | Three cycles. |
| Good-risk disease with bleomycin contraindication | EP ×4 | Bleomycin cannot be used. | Four cycles. |
| Intermediate-risk seminoma or intermediate-/poor-risk NSGCT | BEP ×4 | IGCCCG risk group. | Four cycles. |
| Bleomycin contraindication in intermediate-/poor-risk disease | VIP ×4 with primary G-CSF | Expert-center management. | Four cycles. |
| Poor-risk NSGCT with unfavorable marker decline after cycle 1 | Germ-cell center referral and consideration of marker-directed intensification | GETUG-13 selected population. | Protocol-driven. |

| [IGCCCG Update Consortium](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8099402/) | Contemporary metastatic NSGCT receiving first-line cisplatin/etoposide-based therapy | Prognostic cohort | No randomized comparator | Five-year PFS/OS prognosis | Good/intermediate/poor-risk NSGCT 5-year OS: 96%/89%/67%. | Prognostic cohort, not regimen-comparison evidence. |

## Biomarkers

| Marker or feature | Role now | Practical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| AFP, hCG, and LDH | IGCCCG classification and response assessment | Obtain prechemotherapy values; pure seminoma should not produce AFP. |
| Early marker decline | Risk refinement in poor-risk disease | Marker-directed intensification was studied in a selected GETUG-13 population. |
| FDG-PET | Selected seminoma residual-mass assessment | Do not use for NSGCT residual disease; a single positive seminoma PET does not establish viable cancer. |

## Upcoming trial results

| Trial | Why it may change care | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| [P3BEP](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02582697) | Tests accelerated versus standard BEP ×4 in intermediate-/poor-risk metastatic GCT. | Recruiting phase 3; no comparative efficacy result. |
| [TIGER](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02375204) | Compares first-salvage TIP with TI-CE high-dose chemotherapy/autologous transplant. | Active, not recruiting; no efficacy result identified. |

## Toxicity

| Regimen and comparator | Trial-level safety outcomes | Selected named toxicities | Counseling focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dose-dense intensification versus continued BEP, [GETUG-13](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39167741/) | Toxic death 1% in each arm. | Grade 3–4 neurotoxicity 7% vs 1%; grade 1–2 febrile neutropenia 17% vs 18%. | Urgent contact for fever, infection, new/worsening neuropathy, dyspnea, dehydration, or severe vomiting. |
| Standard BEP or VIP | No population-matched primary source with a complete named-event table was retrieved. | Do not assign unverified rates. | Counsel on cisplatin renal/hearing/neurologic effects, bleomycin pulmonary risk, myelosuppression, infertility, and survivorship risks. |
