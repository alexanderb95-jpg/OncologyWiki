#metastaticgct

- Counseling: Reviewed metastatic germ-cell tumor, prechemotherapy AFP/hCG/LDH, IGCCCG prognosis group, and the importance of germ-cell specialist coordination and fertility preservation.
- Counseling: Reviewed sequence: use BEP ×3 for good-risk disease, EP ×4 when bleomycin is contraindicated, BEP ×4 for intermediate/poor-risk disease, VIP ×4 with primary G-CSF when bleomycin cannot be used, and consider marker-directed intensification only for the selected poor-risk unfavorable-decline population. ***
- Monitoring: Check CBC, renal function, hearing, neuropathy, nausea/vomiting, and fertility plan with all cisplatin/etoposide regimens; monitor pulmonary symptoms with bleomycin, hemorrhagic-cystitis symptoms with ifosfamide, and infection risk with VIP/intensification. Contact oncology urgently for fever, new dyspnea, worsening neuropathy, dehydration, hematuria, confusion, or uncontrolled vomiting. ***
- Decision: *** selects BEP / EP / VIP / high-volume germ-cell center referral / trial consideration.
