# Prostate → Localized high-risk

Last reviewed: 2026-09-21
Next review: 2026-12-20
Owner: GU clinic
Purpose: Shared decision support for localized high-risk or locally advanced prostate cancer
Status: current — local therapy is the foundation; systemic intensification evidence applies to a defined very-high-risk/cN1 population

## Who this applies to

| Population | Direct-evidence boundary | Not established by the pivotal data |
|---|---|---|
| High-risk or locally advanced, nonmetastatic prostate cancer | Confirm clinical stage, PSA, Grade Group, conventional staging, life expectancy, urinary function, and treatment priorities. | Do not equate a guideline high-risk label with every trial population. |
| STAMPEDE-like high-risk M0 disease | Conventional-imaging M0, WHO performance status 0–2, and cN1 or cN0 with at least two of cT3–4, Gleason 8–10, and PSA ≥40 ng/mL. | The abiraterone result should not be extrapolated to every high-risk patient. |

## Standard options

| Clinical context | Option | Key inclusion | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Localized high-risk disease | Radical prostatectomy within planned multimodality care | Surgical fitness and a plan for pathology-directed postoperative management. | Procedure plus risk-adapted follow-up. |
| Locally advanced or high-risk disease selecting radiation | Definitive radiotherapy plus long-course ADT | Localized/locally advanced disease; integrate urinary, bowel, bone, metabolic, and cardiovascular risks. | EORTC 22961 compared 6 months with 3 years of androgen suppression. |
| STAMPEDE-like very-high-risk or cN1 M0 disease receiving ADT plus radiotherapy | Add abiraterone plus prednisolone | Exact STAMPEDE high-risk M0 eligibility required. | ADT 3 years plus abiraterone/prednisolone 2 years in the trial. |

## Landmark evidence

<!-- .cross_trial -->
| Trial | Population | Intervention | Comparator | Primary endpoint | Clinically meaningful outcomes | Generalizability-critical differences |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [EORTC 22961](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19516032/), N=970 | Locally advanced disease after radiotherapy plus 6 months androgen suppression | Continue androgen suppression for 2.5 more years | Stop androgen suppression | Overall survival | Five-year mortality 15.2% with long-course versus 19.0% with short-course suppression; short-course HR for death 1.42. | Directly tests duration after radiotherapy, not surgery or abiraterone intensification. |
| [STAMPEDE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8811484/), N=1,974 | High-risk nonmetastatic disease on ADT; radiotherapy planned/required by protocol context | ADT plus abiraterone/prednisolone | ADT alone | Metastasis-free survival | Six-year MFS 82% vs 69%; MFS HR 0.53. OS HR 0.60. | Very-high-risk/cN1 eligibility; not a comparison of local-treatment modalities. |

## Biomarkers

| Marker or feature | Role now | Practical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Grade Group, PSA, clinical stage, and conventional imaging | Risk and treatment selection | Define clinical risk; confirm before applying trial criteria. |
| Decipher | Investigational for tailoring ADT duration in this setting | NRG-GU009 is testing its use; do not substitute it for trial eligibility outside a protocol. |
| Germline and somatic testing | Hereditary-risk and later-treatment planning | Does not select standard local therapy in the source pack. |

## Upcoming trial results

| Trial | Why it may change care | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| [NRG-GU009 / PREDICT-RT](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04513717) | Tests Decipher-directed ADT duration and apalutamide intensification with radiotherapy in high-risk disease. | Phase 3, active not recruiting; MFS primary endpoint; no efficacy results. |

## Toxicity

| Regimen and comparator | Trial-level safety outcomes | Selected named toxicities | Counseling focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADT plus abiraterone/prednisolone versus ADT, [STAMPEDE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8811484/) | Grade ≥3 AEs: 37% vs 29%. | Grade ≥3 hypertension 5% vs 1%; grade ≥3/4 ALT elevation 6% vs <1%. | Monitor blood pressure and hepatic tests; promptly report severe headache, chest symptoms, jaundice, or marked fatigue. |
| Radiotherapy plus androgen suppression, [EORTC 22961](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19516032/) | The retrieved abstract does not supply a comparable numerical toxicity table. | Do not add unverified named-event percentages. | Counsel on urinary/bowel change, sexual function, hot flashes, fatigue, bone loss, metabolic and cardiovascular effects. |
