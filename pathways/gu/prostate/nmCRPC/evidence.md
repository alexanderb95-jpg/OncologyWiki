# Prostate → nmCRPC

Last reviewed: 2026-09-21
Next review: 2026-12-20
Owner: GU clinic
Purpose: Shared decision support for high-risk nonmetastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer
Status: current — three placebo-controlled phase 3 trials support androgen-receptor pathway inhibition with continued ADT in the conventional-imaging, high-risk population

## Who this applies to

### Pivotal-trial eligibility and decision boundary

| Trial | Eligible population | Key imaging and clinical boundary | Outside direct pivotal evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [PROSPER](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8288034/) | Castration-resistant adenocarcinoma, PSADT ≤10 months, continued ADT | CT/MRI and radionuclide bone scan without metastases; seizure history or predisposition was excluded. | PSADT >10 months, prior or current conventional-imaging metastases, or seizure-risk population. |
| [SPARTAN](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1715546) | PSADT ≤10 months, continued ADT | Technetium bone scan and CT without distant metastases; pelvic N1 nodes <2 cm below the aortic bifurcation were allowed. | Conventional-imaging distant metastases or disease outside the trial definition. |
| [ARAMIS](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1815671) | PSADT ≤10 months, PSA ≥2 ng/mL, ECOG 0–1, continued ADT | CT/MRI and radionuclide bone scan without detectable or prior metastases; pelvic nodes <2 cm below the aortic bifurcation were allowed. | ECOG status outside the study population, conventional-imaging metastases, or disease outside the trial definition. |

- PSMA-PET-only disease, PSA doubling time >10 months, and failure to meet the castration definition lack direct pivotal-trial efficacy estimates.
- Confirm castrate testosterone, PSA kinetics, conventional imaging, prior androgen-receptor pathway inhibitor exposure, falls/fracture risk, blood pressure, cognition, cardiovascular comorbidity, and patient priorities before selecting treatment.

## Standard options

| Clinical group | Option | Evidence boundary | Typical course |
|---|---|---|---|
| High-risk nmCRPC within a pivotal-trial population | Enzalutamide plus continued ADT | PROSPER used conventional-imaging nonmetastatic disease and PSADT ≤10 months. | Enzalutamide 160 mg orally daily with continued ADT; duration in the source pack is not stated. |
| High-risk nmCRPC within a pivotal-trial population | Apalutamide plus continued ADT | SPARTAN used conventional-imaging nonmetastatic disease and PSADT ≤10 months. | Apalutamide 240 mg orally daily with continued ADT; duration in the source pack is not stated. |
| High-risk nmCRPC within a pivotal-trial population | Darolutamide plus continued ADT | ARAMIS used conventional-imaging nonmetastatic disease and PSADT ≤10 months. | Darolutamide 600 mg orally twice daily with food and continued ADT; duration in the source pack is not stated. |
| Outside the direct pivotal population or unable to use an ARPI | Individualized surveillance, supportive management, or clinical-trial evaluation | Do not extrapolate the three phase 3 efficacy estimates to this group. | Individualized. |

The pivotal trials do not provide a head-to-head comparison or ranking of apalutamide, darolutamide, and enzalutamide.

## Therapy sequencing

| Disease state | Treatment order | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| High-risk nmCRPC on conventional imaging | Continue ADT → add one of enzalutamide, apalutamide, or darolutamide. | The pivotal trials do not compare the agents head-to-head and require PSADT ≤10 months with conventional-imaging M0 disease. |
| PSMA-PET-only disease or PSADT >10 months | Individualize surveillance, staging, or trial evaluation. | Direct phase 3 treatment-selection evidence is absent for these states. |
| Conventional-imaging metastasis or progression while castrate | Continue ADT and transition to the mCRPC pathway with prior ARPI exposure documented. | Do not presume sequential ARPI benefit from these trials. |

## Landmark evidence

The phase 3 studies were placebo-controlled, conventional-imaging nmCRPC trials. The final OS time points differ—median OS in PROSPER and SPARTAN versus 3-year OS in ARAMIS—and must not be compared across trials.

## Biomarkers

| Marker or feature | Role now | Practical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| PSA doubling time | Trial risk-enrichment and eligibility factor | A PSADT ≤10 months defined the pivotal-trial population; it is not an agent-selecting biomarker. |
| Conventional imaging | Disease-state classification boundary | The phase 3 evidence used conventional CT/MRI and bone imaging to define nonmetastatic disease. |
| Molecular testing | No validated treatment selector | The pivotal eligibility texts did not report molecular enrollment criteria that select among the three agents. |
| PSMA PET | Staging information, not a validated treatment selector | In one retrospective 200-patient high-risk cohort, PSMA PET found M1 disease in 55% despite negative conventional imaging; prospective treatment-guidance value remains unestablished. [Fendler et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31511295/) |

## Upcoming trial results

No new practice-changing randomized result was identified in the source pack.

| Trial or publication | Why it may change care | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| [PSMACare](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05849298), NCT05849298 | Tests lutetium-177 vipivotide tetraxetan alone or with an androgen-receptor pathway inhibitor in PSMA-PET-positive, conventional-imaging-M0 CRPC; the registry permits PET-only M1 disease. | Active, not recruiting; randomized noncomparative phase 2; actual N=49; no posted outcomes. |
| [DAROL](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41176520/) | Observational real-world darolutamide experience may inform delivery, but does not compare agents or establish a new treatment standard. | Peer-reviewed observational interim report. |

## Toxicity

| Regimen and comparator | Trial-level safety outcomes | Selected named toxicities | Counseling focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enzalutamide + ADT versus placebo + ADT, [PROSPER](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8288034/) | Grade ≥3 AEs: 31% vs 23%; discontinuation due to AEs: 9% vs 6%. | Fatigue 33% vs 14%; hypertension 12% vs 5%; falls 11% vs 4%; dizziness 10% vs 4%; falls/nonpathologic fractures combined 17% vs 8%. | Contact oncology for marked fatigue, new dizziness, a fall, possible fracture, or blood-pressure concerns; interruption or discontinuation may be needed. |
| Apalutamide + ADT versus placebo + ADT, [SPARTAN](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29420164/) | Grade 3–4 AEs: 45.1% vs 34.2%; discontinuation due to AEs: 10.6% vs 7.0%. | Fatigue 30.4% vs 21.1%; hypertension 24.8% vs 19.8%; rash 23.8% vs 5.5%; falls 15.6% vs 9.0%; fracture 11.7% vs 6.5%; hypothyroidism 8.1% vs 2.0%. | Contact oncology for extensive rash, marked fatigue, a fall or suspected fracture, or blood-pressure concerns; treatment interruption or discontinuation may be needed. |
| Darolutamide + ADT versus placebo + ADT, [ARAMIS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30763142/) | Grade 3–4 AEs: 24.7% vs 19.5%; discontinuation due to AEs: 8.9% vs 8.7%. | Fatigue 12.1% vs 8.7%; hypertension 6.6% vs 5.2%. | Contact oncology for worsening fatigue, palpitations, chest symptoms, or blood-pressure concerns; treatment interruption or discontinuation may be needed. |

### Patient counseling and action plan

- Contact oncology promptly for a new extensive rash, marked fatigue, a fall or suspected fracture, marked dizziness, new confusion or memory change, palpitations, chest symptoms, or another concerning treatment effect. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
- Maintain fall and fracture-risk assessment, blood-pressure monitoring, and thyroid assessment where clinically indicated; treatment holds or discontinuation may be required.
