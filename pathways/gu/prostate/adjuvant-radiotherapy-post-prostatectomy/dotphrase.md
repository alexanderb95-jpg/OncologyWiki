- Counseling: Modern trials (ARTISTIC meta-analysis) showed similar 5-year event-free survival with adjuvant vs early-salvage radiotherapy policy (about 89% vs 88%; HR 0.95). RADICALS-RT 10-year freedom from distant metastasis 93% vs 90% (HR 0.68, CI crossed 1). Observation with early salvage at low PSA is preferred for undetectable PSA after adverse pathology.
- Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: PSA every 3–6 months with observation preferred when PSA undetectable; early salvage RT at low PSA rise rather than routine adjuvant RT for the modern-trial population; persistent PSA or pN+ needs separate intensification discussion.
- Monitoring: During radiotherapy, review urinary leakage/obstruction, retention, hematuria, bowel change, and sexual function; if triptorelin is used, also review hot flashes, fatigue, metabolic, and bone effects. Contact the team for urinary retention, heavy hematuria, or new severe bowel symptoms; late urinary and erectile effects can persist.
