#postprostatectomyart

- Counseling: Reviewed adverse pathology after prostatectomy with undetectable PSA. Modern trials support PSA surveillance with early salvage radiotherapy for the studied population; persistent PSA or pN+ disease requires a separate salvage/intensification discussion.
- Monitoring: During radiotherapy, review urinary leakage/obstruction, retention, hematuria, bowel change, and sexual function; if triptorelin is used, also review hot flashes, fatigue, metabolic, and bone effects. Contact the team for urinary retention, heavy hematuria, or new severe bowel symptoms; late urinary and erectile effects can persist.
