- Counseling: Small-cell/neuroendocrine MIBC retrospective data: neoadjuvant chemotherapy before cystectomy associated with downstaging to ≤pT1N0 in about 62% vs 9% without neoadjuvant chemotherapy and longer median OS (159.5 vs 18.3 months)—retrospective, not randomized. Phase 2 small-cell regimen: 14/18 resectable patients downstaged; cT2N0 5-year OS about 80% (single-arm; no randomized comparator). Variant meta-analysis pooled OS HR 0.74 for neoadjuvant chemotherapy versus no neoadjuvant chemotherapy—observational. Expert pathology review required.
- Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: complete planned neoadjuvant cycles when feasible then local consolidation; post-cystectomy chest/abdomen/pelvis CT every 6 months through 36 months then annually through ~60 months (EAU MIBC framework; subtype may warrant longer clinical counseling without a separate numeric grid); adjuvant cisplatin or nivolumab only if no neoadjuvant given and conventional-MIUC criteria apply.
- Counseling: For small-cell/neuroendocrine disease, reviewed preoperative platinum-based systemic therapy → local consolidation. For other variants, reviewed pathology-directed cystectomy planning; adjuvant cisplatin or nivolumab is considered only after cystectomy without neoadjuvant therapy when appropriate. ***
- Monitoring: With platinum-based therapy, check CBC, renal function, neuropathy/hearing, electrolytes, and nausea/vomiting; with nivolumab, review bowel, lung, liver, skin, and endocrine symptoms. Promptly report fever, infection symptoms, bleeding, severe nausea/vomiting, dyspnea, diarrhea, rash, or endocrine symptoms. ***
