- Counseling: Reviewed post-nephrectomy options for higher-risk clear-cell renal cell carcinoma: risk-adapted surveillance versus adjuvant pembrolizumab when KEYNOTE-564 criteria are met.
- Counseling: In KEYNOTE-564, estimated 5-year disease-free survival was 60.9% with pembrolizumab versus 52.2% with placebo; estimated 5-year overall survival was 87.7% versus 82.3%. These results apply to the trial population, not lower-risk or non-clear-cell RCC.
- Counseling: For M1 NED, metastases were completely resected at nephrectomy or within 1 year. The subgroup was small (7/29 versus 19/29 DFS events at the 30-month analysis), so we will use multidisciplinary review rather than treat this as a definitive standalone estimate.
- Counseling: Reviewed immune-related risks, including thyroid, adrenal or pituitary, liver, bowel, lung, kidney, and diabetes toxicity; some effects can persist, and serious symptoms need urgent assessment. We discussed that treatment can require holding therapy, corticosteroids, and endocrine replacement.
- Counseling: Pembrolizumab plus belzutifan improved DFS in LITESPARK-022 but has immature overall-survival data and more grade 3 or higher toxicity; it is not the default regimen discussed today.
