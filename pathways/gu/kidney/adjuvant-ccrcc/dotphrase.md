- Counseling: Reviewed pathology-based recurrence risk after nephrectomy: clear-cell component ***, pT/pN stage ***, grade/sarcomatoid features ***, margins ***, and M1 NED status ***. This patient does / does not meet KEYNOTE-564 eligibility: ***.
- Counseling: Reviewed adjuvant pembrolizumab and risk-adapted surveillance. In KEYNOTE-564, pembrolizumab was compared with placebo after surgery, not with surveillance. At 5 years, estimated disease-free survival was 60.9% with pembrolizumab versus 52.2% with placebo, and overall survival was 87.7% versus 82.3%. These results apply to the trial population and do not establish benefit in lower-risk or non-clear-cell RCC.
- Counseling: For M1 NED, reviewed that the trial required complete resection of the primary tumor and a solid, isolated soft-tissue metastasis at nephrectomy or within 1 year. The subgroup was small; multidisciplinary review is needed rather than a standalone efficacy estimate.
- Counseling: Reviewed pembrolizumab toxicity. In KEYNOTE-564, grade ≥3 all-cause AEs occurred in 32% versus 18%, immune-mediated AEs in 36% versus 7%, and AEs led to discontinuation in 21% versus 2%, with pembrolizumab versus placebo. Higher-grade treatment-related and immune toxicities may require a treatment hold, corticosteroids, or specialty support.
- Counseling: Reviewed immune-related risks: thyroid, adrenal/pituitary, liver, bowel, lung, kidney, skin, and diabetes toxicity. Call the oncology team promptly for new/worsening diarrhea or severe abdominal pain, jaundice/dark urine, cough or shortness of breath, marked fatigue/dizziness, or change in urination. Seek urgent care for severe breathing difficulty. Some endocrine effects need long-term hormone replacement.
- Counseling: If considering pembrolizumab plus belzutifan, reviewed that it is FDA-labeled in the US for eligible clear-cell RCC. LITESPARK-022 reported grade ≥3 treatment-emergent AEs in 52.1% versus 30.2% with pembrolizumab plus placebo; selected grade ≥3 events included anemia (12.1% versus 0.4%) and hypoxia (4.6% versus 0%). Overall-survival data were immature; the combination is not presented as OS-proven.
- Counseling: Reviewed belzutifan-specific anemia and hypoxia. Obtain CBC/hemoglobin and oxygen saturation before and during therapy; report shortness of breath or hypoxia symptoms immediately. The oncology team will direct holds, dose reductions, transfusion/supportive care, or discontinuation—do not independently skip, reduce, or restart treatment.
- Counseling: Reviewed reproductive risk with belzutifan. Confirm pregnancy testing *** when applicable and nonhormonal contraception counseling ***; belzutifan can harm a fetus and may make some hormonal contraception less effective. Follow the current regimen-specific labels for the post-treatment interval.
- Counseling: Reviewed that sunitinib remains FDA-labeled after nephrectomy but is not a preferred adjuvant option: the 2026 EAU guideline advises against it after resection of high-risk clear-cell RCC, and S-TRAC reported grade 3 and grade 4 AEs in 48.4% and 12.1%, respectively.
- Decision: *** elects pembrolizumab / pembrolizumab plus belzutifan / risk-adapted surveillance / clinical-trial evaluation after shared decision-making.
- Labs/NGS: Confirmed histology *** and KEYNOTE-564 risk features ***. Obtain CBC with hemoglobin, CMP with creatinine and liver tests, and thyroid testing per institutional protocol. For belzutifan, document baseline oxygen saturation *** and monitor hemoglobin and oxygen saturation per label and local protocol. Tumor NGS and ctDNA are not required to select adjuvant therapy.
- Procedures: *** postoperative restaging and multidisciplinary review completed / planned. For non-clear-cell histology or disease outside KEYNOTE-564 criteria, favor surveillance or clinical-trial review rather than extrapolating pembrolizumab benefit.
- Medications: If selected, pembrolizumab monotherapy is 200 mg IV every 3 weeks for up to 17 cycles. Pembrolizumab plus belzutifan is pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks or 400 mg IV every 6 weeks plus belzutifan 120 mg orally daily, for up to 1 year; confirm current FDA label and local formulary.
- Referrals: *** medical oncology / endocrinology / nephrology / clinical-trial consultation, as indicated.
- Follow-up: *** risk-adapted chest and abdominal surveillance; at each visit review new immune-related symptoms, renal function, thyroid status, and—if belzutifan is used—hemoglobin and oxygen saturation.
