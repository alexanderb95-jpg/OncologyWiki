- Counseling: Reviewed post-nephrectomy options for higher-risk clear-cell renal cell carcinoma: risk-adapted surveillance, adjuvant pembrolizumab, and—when applicable in the US—pembrolizumab plus belzutifan.
- Counseling: In KEYNOTE-564, estimated 5-year disease-free survival was 60.9% with pembrolizumab versus 52.2% with placebo; estimated 5-year overall survival was 87.7% versus 82.3%. These results apply to the trial population, not lower-risk or non-clear-cell RCC.
- Counseling: For M1 NED, metastases were completely resected at nephrectomy or within 1 year. The subgroup was small (7/29 versus 19/29 DFS events at the 30-month analysis), so we will use multidisciplinary review rather than treat this as a definitive standalone estimate.
- Counseling: Reviewed immune-related risks, including thyroid, adrenal or pituitary, liver, bowel, lung, kidney, and diabetes toxicity; some effects can persist, and serious symptoms need urgent assessment. We discussed that treatment can require holding therapy, corticosteroids, and endocrine replacement.
- Counseling: Pembrolizumab plus belzutifan is FDA-approved in the US for eligible clear-cell RCC after nephrectomy. LITESPARK-022 showed a DFS benefit, while overall-survival data remain immature and grade 3 or higher toxicity was more frequent than with pembrolizumab plus placebo.
- Decision: *** elects pembrolizumab / pembrolizumab plus belzutifan / risk-adapted surveillance / clinical-trial evaluation after shared decision-making.
- Labs/NGS: Confirmed histology *** and KEYNOTE-564 or LITESPARK-022 risk features ***. Obtain CBC, CMP with creatinine and liver tests, and thyroid testing per institutional protocol. For belzutifan, follow the current label for regimen-specific monitoring and dose modification. Tumor NGS is not required to select adjuvant therapy.
- Procedures: *** postoperative restaging and multidisciplinary review completed / planned. For non-clear-cell histology, favor surveillance or clinical-trial review rather than extrapolating KEYNOTE-564.
- Medications: If selected, pembrolizumab monotherapy is 200 mg IV every 3 weeks for up to 17 cycles. Pembrolizumab plus belzutifan is pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks or 400 mg IV every 6 weeks plus belzutifan 120 mg orally daily, for up to 1 year; confirm the current FDA label and local formulary.
- Referrals: *** medical oncology / endocrinology / nephrology / clinical-trial consultation, as indicated.
- Follow-up: *** risk-adapted chest and abdominal surveillance schedule; review symptoms and renal function at each visit.
