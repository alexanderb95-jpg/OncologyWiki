#utucadjuvant

- Counseling: POUT adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum after RNU: 3-year disease-free survival about 71 in 100 vs 46 in 100 (HR 0.45); 5-year DFS about 62% vs 45% (HR 0.55). Five-year OS 66% vs 57%; adjusted OS HR 0.76 (CI crossed 1)—DFS is the established benefit. Nivolumab adjuvant data are from all-MIUC CheckMate 274, not a verified UTUC subgroup estimate.
- Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: four adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum cycles then stop; CT every 6 months ×2 years then annually plus bladder-recurrence cystoscopy; single postoperative intravesical chemotherapy when indicated; metastatic recurrence → systemic pathway.
- Counseling: Reviewed sequence: post-RNU → four cycles of gemcitabine–platinum; use cisplatin at POUT GFR ≥50 and carboplatin at GFR 30–49. Single intravesical chemotherapy is a separate bladder-recurrence intervention; nivolumab for platinum-ineligible disease requires an explicit all-MIUC evidence discussion. ***
- Monitoring: With gemcitabine–platinum, check CBC, renal function, electrolytes, neuropathy/hearing, and nausea/vomiting. With nivolumab, review bowel, lung, liver, skin, and endocrine symptoms; after intravesical treatment, review fever, worsening urinary symptoms, dysuria, and hematuria. Contact oncology urgently for fever, infection symptoms, bleeding, uncontrolled vomiting, dehydration, or concerning immune symptoms. ***
- Decision: *** selects adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum / surveillance / further recovery or trial assessment.
