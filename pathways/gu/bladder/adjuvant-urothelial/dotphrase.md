#Adjuvant urothelial carcinoma
- Counseling: CheckMate 274 adjuvant nivolumab improved disease-free survival versus placebo in high-risk MIUC (DFS HR 0.70 vs placebo). AMBASSADOR pembrolizumab improved DFS versus observation (HR 0.73 vs observation); overall survival was not reported in the retrieved abstract. IMvigor011 ctDNA MRD-positive adjuvant atezolizumab improved DFS (HR 0.64 vs placebo) and OS (HR 0.59 vs placebo)—distinct from negative unselected IMvigor010. Prior perioperative EV/pembro or NIAGARA changes who still needs separate adjuvant ICI.
- Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: CT every 3–6 months ×2 years then every 6–12 months; adjuvant PD-1 for up to 1 year then stop unless progression/toxicity; ctDNA-negative patients should not receive unselected adjuvant atezolizumab; recurrence → metastatic urothelial pathway.
- Monitoring: With adjuvant nivolumab, pembrolizumab, or atezolizumab, review new diarrhea/abdominal pain, cough/dyspnea, jaundice, rash, marked fatigue, palpitations, or temperature/weight intolerance before every treatment. With perioperative EV plus pembrolizumab, additionally review numbness/weakness, vision change, thirst/polyuria, and rash; with durvalumab plus GC, monitor CBC, renal function, electrolytes, neuropathy/hearing, and immune symptoms.
