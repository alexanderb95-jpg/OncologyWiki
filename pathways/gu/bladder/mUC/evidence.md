# Bladder / urothelial → Metastatic urothelial carcinoma

Last reviewed: 2026-09-22
Next review: 2026-12-21
Owner: GU clinic
Purpose: Disease → setting page for locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma
Status: clinic-ready — EV+pembro is the 1L OS-proven default for many; FGFR/HER2 as labeled

## Who this applies to
- Unresectable locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma[^10][^11].
- Record cisplatin eligibility, prior perioperative/adjuvant IO or EV, sites (liver), neuropathy, glycemic status, FGFR3, HER2 IHC, PD-L1 (less central for EV+pembro)[^1][^7][^8][^10].

## Standard options

| Clinical context | Option | Key inclusion | Typical course |
|---|---|---|---|
| Untreated la/mUC | EV plus pembrolizumab[^1] | Assess neuropathy, glucose, organ function, and prior perioperative exposure[^7]. | EV days 1 and 8 plus pembrolizumab every 3 weeks in EV-302[^1]. |
| Cisplatin-eligible untreated la/mUC not using EV plus pembrolizumab | Nivolumab plus GC[^2] | CheckMate 901-type cisplatin fitness[^2]. | GC induction then nivolumab maintenance per trial[^2]. |
| No progression after platinum | Avelumab maintenance[^3] | JAVELIN Bladder 100 population[^3]. | Continue until progression or unacceptable toxicity[^3]. |
| Subsequent therapy | EV, biomarker-directed therapy, trial, or supportive care[^4][^5][^10] | Match prior therapy and FGFR/HER2 status[^5][^8]. | Regimen-specific[^7][^8]. |
- 1L most patients: EV 1.25 mg/kg d1,8 + pembrolizumab 200 mg d1 q3wk (EV-302)[^1].
- 1L cisplatin-eligible if not using EV+pembro: nivo 360 mg + GC q3wk ×6 then nivo 480 mg q4wk (CheckMate 901)[^2].
- 1L platinum then, if no PD: maintenance avelumab (JAVELIN)[^3]. This pathway is largely displaced by EV+pembro but still applies if platinum was already given[^1][^3].
- Post platinum + PD-(L)1: EV monotherapy (EV-301)[^4].
- FGFR3-altered after ≥1 line including PD-(L)1: erdafitinib (THOR cohort 1)[^5]. Do not use erdafitinib in PD-(L)1-naive eligible patients (FDA limitation; THOR cohort 2 OS HR 1.18 vs pembro)[^8].
- HER2-expressing: discuss T-DXd / other HER2 ADCs as trial or label-permitted tumor-agnostic options; RC48-C016 is not a verified US SOC in this pack[^10][^11].
- After platinum, pembrolizumab vs chemo (KEYNOTE-045) if no prior PD-1[^10][^11].
- Supportive care / trial[^10][^11].

## Therapy sequencing

| Starting point or prior exposure | Next treatment order | Branch point / evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Untreated la/mUC | EV plus pembrolizumab is the default sequence in this page for many patients[^1]. | Record baseline neuropathy, glycemic status, and prior perioperative EV or immune-checkpoint inhibitor before starting[^7]. |
| Untreated cisplatin-eligible disease when EV plus pembrolizumab is not used | Nivolumab plus GC induction → nivolumab maintenance[^2]. | CheckMate 901 is a cisplatin-eligible regimen; it is not the platinum-induction → avelumab sequence[^2][^3]. |
| Platinum induction without progression | Start avelumab maintenance rather than observation alone[^3]. | Applies after 4–6 cycles of gemcitabine plus cisplatin or carboplatin in JAVELIN Bladder 100[^3]. |
| Progression after platinum plus PD-(L)1 without prior EV | EV monotherapy[^4]. | EV-301 directly studied this post-platinum, post-PD-(L)1 setting[^4]. |
| Progression after EV plus pembrolizumab | Reassess FGFR3/HER2 and trial eligibility; use a biomarker-matched or non-cross-resistant option when its own label criteria are met[^5][^8][^10]. | A preferred post-EV plus pembrolizumab sequence is not established by a randomized phase 3 trial in this source set; do not assume EV monotherapy is evidence-based after EV-containing first line[^1][^10]. |
| FGFR3/2-altered disease after PD-(L)1 | Erdafitinib[^5]. | THOR cohort 1 supports this after prior PD-(L)1; it does not support substituting erdafitinib for an eligible first PD-1 pathway[^5][^8]. |

## Surveillance and treatment de-escalation

| Phase | Monitoring cadence | Stop, hold, or de-escalate | Re-escalation / next line |
|---|---|---|---|
| On first-line EV plus pembrolizumab or platinum-ICI | Imaging every 9–12 weeks during active treatment; CBC, CMP, glucose, and neuropathy assessment on EV; immune-symptom review each cycle[^7][^10][^11]. | Continue until RECIST progression or unacceptable toxicity; hold EV for neuropathy grade ≥2 or hyperglycemia; hold pembrolizumab for immune events[^7]. Maintenance avelumab (JAVELIN) continues until progression up to 2 years[^3]. | Progression → EV monotherapy if post-platinum/post-PD-(L)1 without prior EV; FGFR/HER2-directed therapy; trial; supportive care[^4][^5][^10]. |
| After platinum without progression | Imaging every 12 weeks on avelumab maintenance[^3][^10]. | Stop avelumab at progression or 2-year cap per trial[^3]. | Progression → second-line options per sequencing table[^4][^5][^10]. |

- EAU/NCCN recommend cross-sectional imaging for metastatic urothelial carcinoma on treatment and at progression; cystoscopy is not central for metastatic disease but remains relevant for bladder-primary local symptoms[^10][^11].

## Biomarkers
- FGFR3 (companion diagnostic) for erdafitinib after PD-(L)1[^5][^8].
- HER2 IHC: RC48-C016 used 1+/2+/3+; T-DXd greatest benefit in IHC 3+ across tumors (DESTINY-PanTumor02) — bladder-only numbers not in sources[^10][^11].
- PD-L1: not the selector for EV+pembro 1L in EV-302 as reported here[^1].
- NGS for FGFR and other trial targets[^5][^10].

## Upcoming research

Field direction after EV plus pembrolizumab as first-line Nectin-4 ADC plus PD-1 standard: competing HER2-directed vedotin ADCs with PD-1 in biomarker-selected untreated disease, next-generation Nectin-4 ADCs with non-MMAE payloads after enfortumab exposure, and still-unsettled post-EV plus pembrolizumab sequencing (FGFR, HER2, taxane, TROP2) without a randomized preferred line in this source set[^1][^10][^13][^16].

| Program | Why it may change care | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| [SGNDV-001 / DV-001](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05911295) (NCT05911295) | Phase 3 disitamab vedotin (HER2 ADC) plus pembrolizumab versus gemcitabine–platinum in untreated HER2 IHC ≥1+ la/mUC; dual primary BICR PFS and OS[^13][^14][^15]. | Ongoing (active, not recruiting); design manuscript peer-reviewed; efficacy results pending[^13][^14]. |
| [RC48-C016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41124210/) (NCT05302284) | Phase 3 disitamab vedotin plus toripalimab versus chemotherapy in untreated HER2 IHC 1+/2+/3+ la/mUC; peer-reviewed PFS/OS benefit versus chemotherapy[^18]. | Peer-reviewed (NEJM); US regulatory status as a first-line standard is not established in this pack[^10][^11][^18]. |
| [NEXUS-01](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06465069) (NCT06465069) | Phase 1 next-generation Nectin-4 ADC LY4052031 (camptothecin/topo-I payload) in advanced UC, including post-enfortumab cohorts[^16][^17]. | Ongoing phase 1 (recruiting); early activity congress-reported (ASCO 2026 / UroToday); not practice-changing yet[^16][^17]. |
| Post–EV plus pembrolizumab sequencing | Preferred order among FGFR-directed therapy, HER2 options, taxane, and TROP2 ADC after first-line EV plus pembrolizumab[^1][^5][^9][^10]. | No randomized phase 3 preferred sequence in this source set; treat as an evidence gap[^1][^10]. |


## Toxicity

| Regimen and comparator | Trial-level safety outcomes | Counseling focus |
|---|---|---|
| EV plus pembrolizumab versus platinum chemotherapy, EV-302 | Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 55.9% vs 69.5%[^1]. | Report progressive neuropathy, rash, visual symptoms, hyperglycemia symptoms, cough/dyspnea, diarrhea, or endocrine symptoms promptly[^1][^7]. |
| Nivolumab plus GC versus GC, CheckMate 901 | Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 61.8% vs 51.7%; discontinuation 21.1% vs 17.4%[^2]. | Review cytopenias, infection, renal function, neuropathy, and immune toxicity[^2]. |
| Avelumab maintenance plus BSC, JAVELIN Bladder 100 | Long-term treatment-related-AE discontinuation 10.2%; no new safety signal with ≥2 years of follow-up[^3]. | Report new diarrhea, abdominal pain, cough/dyspnea, rash, jaundice, or endocrine symptoms promptly[^3]. |
| EV monotherapy versus chemotherapy, EV-301 | Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 52.4% vs 50.5%[^4]. | Report progressive neuropathy, rash, visual symptoms, or hyperglycemia symptoms promptly[^4][^7]. |
| Erdafitinib versus chemotherapy, THOR cohort 1 | Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs 45.9% vs 46.4%[^5]. | Review hyperphosphatemia, nail/skin, and ocular symptoms; prompt ophthalmology assessment for visual change[^5][^6]. |
| Sacituzumab govitecan versus chemotherapy, TROPiCS-04 | Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 67% vs 35%; primary OS endpoint not met[^9]. | Urgent contact for fever/infection, severe diarrhea, dehydration, or marked fatigue[^9]. |
| Pembrolizumab or a tumor-agnostic HER2-directed agent, when an individual indication applies | This page does not contain a comparable urothelial trial safety table for each later-line use[^10][^11]. | Follow the selected agent's label and trial protocol; report immune-toxicity symptoms or new cough/dyspnea promptly[^10][^11]. |

### On-treatment monitoring

| SOC/option regimen | Hallmark monitoring toxicity and published rate | Patient action / clinic response |
|---|---|---|
| EV plus pembrolizumab | EV-302 FDA label treatment-arm rates (single-arm label rates; no paired comparator % in the retained label table): peripheral neuropathy 67%, grade 3–4 8%; rash 68%, grade 3–4 15%; increased glucose 66%, grade 3–4 14%; dry eye 24%, no grade 3–4 event[^7]. Trial-level grade ≥3 TRAEs 55.9% vs 69.5% with platinum chemotherapy[^1]. | Check sensory and motor symptoms, skin, glucose, and visual symptoms at each treatment review. New or worsening numbness/weakness, thirst/polyuria, rash, or vision change should prompt contact before the next dose[^7]. |
| Nivolumab plus GC | CheckMate 901: anemia 57.2%, grade ≥3 22.0%; neutropenia 30.6%, grade ≥3 18.8%; decreased neutrophils 24.7%, grade ≥3 14.5%; one treatment-related grade-5 sepsis[^2]. | Check CBC and renal function and review fever, neuropathy, hearing change, and immune-mediated diarrhea, dyspnea, hepatitis, or endocrinopathy; fever, dyspnea, or severe diarrhea needs urgent assessment[^2]. |
| Avelumab maintenance | JAVELIN Bladder 100: infusion reaction 10.2%, grade ≥3 0.9%; long-term treatment-related-AE discontinuation 10.2%[^3]. | Premedicate during the first four infusions per label and review thyroid, hepatic, renal, pulmonary, and bowel symptoms during and after treatment; new cough/dyspnea, persistent diarrhea/abdominal pain, jaundice, or marked fatigue should prompt urgent contact[^3]. |
| EV monotherapy | EV-301: peripheral neuropathy 48.0%, grade ≥3 7.4%; hyperglycemia 6.8%, grade ≥3 4.4%; skin reactions 47.3%, grade ≥3 14.9%[^4]. | Monitor sensory symptoms, glucose, and skin from the first cycles; hold and assess for progressive neuropathy, severe rash/blistering, or symptomatic hyperglycemia[^4][^7]. |
| Erdafitinib | THOR cohort 1: hyperphosphatemia 80.0%, grade 3–4 5.2%; central serous retinopathy 17.0%, grade 3–4 2.2%[^5]. | Check phosphate on days 14–21 then monthly; arrange scheduled ophthalmic monitoring and hold treatment for visual change[^5][^6]. |
| Sacituzumab govitecan | TROPiCS-04 reported grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 67%; this page's retrieved primary source did not provide a named rate for neutropenia or diarrhea[^9]. | Check CBC and review bowel symptoms; fever, infection symptoms, severe diarrhea, or dehydration needs urgent contact[^9]. |
| Pembrolizumab after platinum if PD-1–naive | KEYNOTE-045: pruritus 19.5%, grade 3–5 3.1%; hypothyroidism 7.1%, no grade 3–5 event; grade 3–5 pneumonitis 2.3% and colitis 1.1%[^10][^11]. | Check liver enzymes, creatinine, and thyroid function; report diarrhea/abdominal pain, cough/dyspnea, jaundice, rash, or endocrine symptoms promptly[^10][^11]. |
| Tumor-agnostic HER2-directed agent, when an individual indication applies | No urothelial-specific safety rate was retrieved for every tumor-agnostic option listed here[^10][^11]. | Use the selected agent's label-specific monitoring and report its designated serious symptoms promptly[^10][^11]. |
