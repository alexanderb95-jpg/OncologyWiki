#variantbladderperiop

- Counseling: Reviewed variant-histology muscle-invasive urothelial cancer and the need for expert GU-pathology and multidisciplinary review. Discussed that evidence differs by subtype and is mostly nonrandomized.
- Counseling: For small-cell/neuroendocrine disease, reviewed preoperative platinum-based systemic therapy → local consolidation. For other variants, reviewed pathology-directed cystectomy planning; adjuvant cisplatin or nivolumab is considered only after cystectomy without neoadjuvant therapy when appropriate. ***
- Monitoring: With platinum-based therapy, check CBC, renal function, neuropathy/hearing, electrolytes, and nausea/vomiting; with nivolumab, review bowel, lung, liver, skin, and endocrine symptoms. Promptly report fever, infection symptoms, bleeding, severe nausea/vomiting, dyspnea, diarrhea, rash, or endocrine symptoms. ***
- Decision: *** selects pathology confirmation / systemic-therapy and cystectomy planning / another individualized approach / clinical-trial evaluation.
- Labs/NGS: CBC, CMP, renal function, pathology review ***, molecular testing ***.
- Procedures: *** cystectomy/PLND and multidisciplinary review.
- Follow-up: *** toxicity, response, surgical timing, and survivorship plan.
