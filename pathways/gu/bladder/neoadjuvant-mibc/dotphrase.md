#neoadjuvantmibc

- Counseling: EV-303 perioperative EV/pembro in cisplatin-ineligible MIBC: 2-year event-free survival about 75% vs 39% with RC+PLND alone (EFS HR 0.40 vs surgery alone); 2-year OS about 80% vs 63% with RC+PLND alone (OS HR 0.50). EV-304 cisplatin-eligible: 2-year EFS 79.4% vs 66.2% with neoadjuvant GC (HR 0.53 vs GC). NIAGARA durvalumab plus GC: 24-month EFS 67.8% vs 59.8% with neoadjuvant GC alone (HR 0.68 vs GC); 24-month OS 82.2% vs 75.2% with GC alone (HR 0.75). Regimens are distinct perioperative packages, not sequential.
- Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: complete neoadjuvant then cystectomy then protocol adjuvant doses; post-cystectomy CT every 3–6 months ×2–3 years; stop systemic therapy at planned perioperative completion; recurrence → adjuvant page if residual high-risk without prior ICI completion, or metastatic pathway.
- Counseling: Reviewed treatment order: cisplatin-eligible patients may receive perioperative durvalumab plus GC or perioperative EV plus pembrolizumab → cystectomy → the regimen's specified postoperative treatment; cisplatin-ineligible/declining patients have EV-303 support for EV plus pembrolizumab → cystectomy → postoperative EV plus pembrolizumab. These are distinct perioperative strategies, not sequential regimens. ***
- Monitoring: For EV plus pembrolizumab, review numbness/weakness, rash, vision changes, thirst/polyuria, creatinine, and immune symptoms before each dose. The FDA label reports EV-303 treatment-arm rates (single-arm label rates; no paired comparator % in the retained label table): neuropathy 39%, rash 54%, and increased glucose 72%; EV-304 treatment-arm rates: neuropathy 43%, rash 63%, and hyperglycemia 20%. Trial-level grade ≥3 AEs were 71.3% vs 45.9% with RC+PLND alone (EV-303) and 75.7% vs 67.2% with neoadjuvant GC (EV-304). For durvalumab plus GC, monitor CBC, renal function, electrolytes, neuropathy/hearing, and immune symptoms; NIAGARA grade 3–4 TRAEs 40.6% vs 40.9% with GC alone. Promptly report rash, numbness/weakness, vision changes, thirst/polyuria, cough/dyspnea, diarrhea, fever, or other concerning treatment effects. ***
- Decision: *** selects perioperative EV plus pembrolizumab / perioperative durvalumab plus GC / another cystectomy-anchored plan / bladder-preservation evaluation.
- Labs/NGS: CBC, CMP, renal function, glucose, baseline neuropathy/hearing assessment ***.
- Procedures: *** urinary obstruction relief, restaging, cystectomy/PLND planning, and multidisciplinary review.
- Follow-up: *** toxicity review, surgical timing, and postoperative treatment planning.
