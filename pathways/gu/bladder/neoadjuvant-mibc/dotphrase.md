#neoadjuvantmibc

- Counseling: Reviewed resectable muscle-invasive bladder cancer, cystectomy anchoring, and perioperative options based on cisplatin eligibility, pathology, renal function after obstruction relief, neuropathy, hearing, performance status, and patient priorities.
- Counseling: Reviewed treatment order: cisplatin-eligible patients may receive perioperative durvalumab plus GC or perioperative EV plus pembrolizumab → cystectomy → the regimen's specified postoperative treatment; cisplatin-ineligible/declining patients have EV-303 support for EV plus pembrolizumab → cystectomy → postoperative EV plus pembrolizumab. These are distinct perioperative strategies, not sequential regimens. ***
- Monitoring: For EV plus pembrolizumab, review numbness/weakness, rash, vision changes, thirst/polyuria, and immune symptoms before each dose; EV-303/EV-304 did not report comparable named perioperative neuropathy or hyperglycemia rates in the retrieved primary reports. For durvalumab plus GC, monitor CBC, renal function, electrolytes, neuropathy/hearing, and immune symptoms. Promptly report rash, numbness/weakness, vision changes, thirst/polyuria, cough/dyspnea, diarrhea, fever, or other concerning treatment effects. ***
- Decision: *** selects perioperative EV plus pembrolizumab / perioperative durvalumab plus GC / another cystectomy-anchored plan / bladder-preservation evaluation.
- Labs/NGS: CBC, CMP, renal function, glucose, baseline neuropathy/hearing assessment ***.
- Procedures: *** urinary obstruction relief, restaging, cystectomy/PLND planning, and multidisciplinary review.
- Follow-up: *** toxicity review, surgical timing, and postoperative treatment planning.
