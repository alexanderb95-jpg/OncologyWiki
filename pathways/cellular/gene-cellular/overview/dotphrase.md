#GeneCellularOverview
- Counseling: Gene and cellular therapy is a heme/onc–wide track (sickle cell gene therapy, heme CAR-T, melanoma TIL, and investigational solid-tumor cellular programs). It is separate from radioligands, ADCs, and small-molecule SOC on disease pages.
- Counseling: SCD gene therapy visit — reviewed risks, benefits, and alternatives of high-dose chemotherapy (myeloablative busulfan) followed by infusion of genetically modified autologous hematopoietic stem cells. Discussed regimen-related morbidity and mortality, short- and long-term consequences of high-dose chemotherapy (including infertility), and the need for supportive care during transplantation including blood-product support and its risks. Reviewed back-up unmodified rescue CD34+ cells for engraftment failure or product compromise. Statistics applicable to the disease and labeled single-arm gene-therapy outcomes were reviewed.
- Counseling: Lyfgenia (lovotibeglogene autotemcel / lovo-cel) — one-time treatment adding functional copies of a modified β-globin gene (βA-T87Q-globin) into the patient’s own HSCs via BB305 lentiviral vector so RBCs can produce anti-sickling HbAT87Q, decreasing HbS proportion with the goal of reducing sickled RBCs, hemolysis, and VOEs. Manufacturing: mobilization (plerixafor) + apheresis → ex vivo transduction → busulfan conditioning → IV infusion. Clinical program includes HGB-206 (NCT02140554), phase 3 HGB-210 (NCT04293185), and long-term follow-up LTF-307 (NCT04628585). Label single-arm Study 1-C: VOE-CR 28/32 (88%) and sVOE-CR 30/32 (94%) between months 6–18 (no randomized comparator). Boxed warning for hematologic malignancy — CBC at least every 6 months for ≥15 years and integration-site testing at months 6 and 12. α-thalassemia trait limitation of use.
- Counseling: Casgevy (exagamglogene autotemcel / exa-cel) — personalized ex vivo CRISPR/Cas9 editing of autologous HSCs at the erythroid BCL11A enhancer to boost fetal hemoglobin (HbF), then return of modified cells after busulfan. Also labeled for transfusion-dependent β-thalassemia. SCD pivotal single-arm Trial 1: VF12 29/31 (93.5%) free of severe VOCs ≥12 months; HF12 30/30 (100%) free of severe-VOC hospitalization ≥12 months (no randomized comparator). Do not use G-CSF for SCD mobilization.
- Counseling: SCD gene-therapy toxicities / management — expect mucositis/stomatitis, febrile neutropenia, prolonged cytopenias, infection and bleeding until engraftment; consider VOD/SOS prophylaxis with busulfan; fertility preservation before conditioning; inpatient transplant-level monitoring then frequent CBC until recovery. Grade 3–4 mucositis about 86 in 100 (38/44) with Casgevy Trial 1 SCD; grade ≥3 stomatitis about 71 in 100 (32/45) in Lyfgenia labeled safety table (single-arm).
- Counseling: Allo-HCT alternative for SCD — when a suitable donor exists, allogeneic transplant can offer prolonged remission/possible cure but with higher modality-specific morbidity than autologous gene therapy, including infection, bleeding, end-organ damage, secondary malignancy, VOD, graft-versus-host disease, graft failure, relapse, and transplant-related mortality. Reviewed GVHD, infection, and VOD prophylaxis. Pivotal Casgevy/Lyfgenia trials excluded patients with an available 10/10 HLA-matched related donor.
- Counseling: LBCL early failure (primary refractory or relapse ≤12 months) — ZUMA-7: axi-cel median EFS 8.3 months vs 2.0 months with salvage chemoimmunotherapy ± ASCT; ORR 83% vs 50% and CR 65% vs 32% with that SOC; longer follow-up median OS not reached vs 31.1 months with SOC. TRANSFORM: liso-cel median EFS 10.1 months vs 2.3 months with salvage→ASCT SOC. BELINDA: tisa-cel did not improve EFS vs SOC in this window.
- Counseling: Myeloma — KarMMa-3: ide-cel median PFS 13.3 months vs 4.4 months with physician’s-choice SOC regimens; ORR 71% vs 42% and CR 39% vs 5% with SOC. CARTITUDE-4: cilta-cel PFS HR 0.26 vs DPd/PVd SOC; 12-month PFS about 76 in 100 vs 49 in 100 with SOC; ORR 84.6% vs 67.3% and ≥CR 73.1% vs 21.8% with SOC.
- Counseling: Other labeled heme CAR-T often rest on single-arm pivotal ORR — say so explicitly when no randomized comparator. CAR-T toxicities: CRS/ICANS (daily monitoring ≥7 days; stay near center ≥2 weeks); prolonged cytopenias; infections; hypogammaglobulinemia (IVIG when IgG <400 mg/dL per cilta-cel-style label guidance); HLH-like presentations; secondary T-cell malignancy warning. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
- Counseling: GU solid tumors — no approved CAR-T/TCR-T/TIL SOC on this wiki. Investigational examples: SURPASS NCT04044859; PSMA/STEAP1 CAR-T (e.g. NCT03089203, NCT06236139); TRAVERSE/ALLO-316 NCT04696731; AB-2100 NCT06245915; BNT211-01 NCT04503278.
- Decision: *** Casgevy / Lyfgenia referral / allo-HCT evaluation if donor available / continue disease-modifying SCD therapy / heme CAR-T if label criteria met / investigational cellular trial / supportive care.
- Labs/NGS: *** CBC with differential, type and screen / transfusion plan, infectious disease screen before apheresis, pregnancy test if applicable, IgG if CAR-T, HLA typing if allo-HCT considered.
- Procedures: *** mobilization/apheresis, fertility preservation, central line, busulfan conditioning admission.
- Meds: *** hydroxyurea hold timing per product; VOD prophylaxis plan; infection prophylaxis; GVHD prophylaxis if allo-HCT.
- Referrals: *** SCD gene-therapy / transplant center, fertility preservation, social work, trial coordinator.
- Follow-up: *** (engraftment labs; Lyfgenia CBC q6 mo ≥15 y + integration-site mo 6/12; SCD organ monitoring; allo-HCT GVHD clinic if applicable).
