#GeneCellularOverview
- Counseling: Gene and cellular therapy is a heme/onc–wide track (sickle cell gene therapy, heme CAR-T, melanoma TIL, and investigational solid-tumor cellular programs). It is separate from radioligands, ADCs, and small-molecule SOC on disease pages.
- Counseling: Sickle cell — Casgevy (exa-cel) is FDA-labeled for patients aged 2 years and older with SCD and recurrent VOCs. In the pivotal single-arm Trial 1, about 94 in 100 evaluable patients (29/31) were free of severe VOCs for ≥12 months (VF12); all evaluable patients (30/30) were free of severe-VOC hospitalization for ≥12 months (HF12). Single-arm; no randomized comparator. Requires myeloablative busulfan; discuss fertility preservation, mucositis/febrile neutropenia, engraftment monitoring, and VOD prophylaxis.
- Counseling: Sickle cell — Lyfgenia (lovo-cel) is FDA-labeled for patients 12 years or older with SCD and a history of VOEs. In single-arm Study 1-C, complete VOE resolution between months 6–18 occurred in 28/32 (88%) and severe-VOE resolution in 30/32 (94%). Single-arm; no randomized comparator. Boxed warning for hematologic malignancy — CBC at least every 6 months for ≥15 years and integration-site testing at months 6 and 12. Busulfan conditioning with infertility risk; α-thalassemia trait limitation of use.
- Counseling: LBCL early failure (primary refractory or relapse ≤12 months) — ZUMA-7: axi-cel median EFS 8.3 months vs 2.0 months with salvage chemoimmunotherapy ± ASCT; ORR 83% vs 50% and CR 65% vs 32% with that SOC; longer follow-up median OS not reached vs 31.1 months with SOC. TRANSFORM: liso-cel median EFS 10.1 months vs 2.3 months with salvage→ASCT SOC. BELINDA: tisa-cel did not improve EFS vs SOC in this window.
- Counseling: Myeloma — KarMMa-3: ide-cel median PFS 13.3 months vs 4.4 months with physician’s-choice SOC regimens; ORR 71% vs 42% and CR 39% vs 5% with SOC. CARTITUDE-4: cilta-cel PFS HR 0.26 vs DPd/PVd SOC; 12-month PFS about 76 in 100 vs 49 in 100 with SOC; ORR 84.6% vs 67.3% and ≥CR 73.1% vs 21.8% with SOC.
- Counseling: Other labeled heme CAR-T (tisa-cel ALL/LBCL/FL; brexu-cel MCL/adult ALL; later-line axi-cel/liso-cel/ide-cel/cilta-cel populations) often rest on single-arm pivotal ORR — say so explicitly when no randomized comparator. Lifileucel melanoma TIL single-arm ORR 31.5% (23/73 at recommended dose); not a GU indication.
- Counseling: CAR-T toxicities — CRS and ICANS (daily monitoring ≥7 days; stay near center ≥2 weeks); prolonged cytopenias; infections; hypogammaglobulinemia (IVIG when IgG <400 mg/dL per cilta-cel-style label guidance); HLH-like presentations; secondary T-cell malignancy warning for CD19/BCMA products with lifelong surveillance. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
- Counseling: GU solid tumors — no approved CAR-T/TCR-T/TIL SOC on this wiki. Investigational referral examples: MAGE-A4 TCR-T (SURPASS / ADP-A2M4CD8, NCT04044859) if HLA-A*02 and MAGE-A4 criteria; PSMA/STEAP1 CAR-T in mCRPC (e.g. NCT03089203, NCT06236139); allogeneic CD70 CAR-T / logic-gated cells in ccRCC after ICI+VEGF (e.g. TRAVERSE/ALLO-316 NCT04696731; AB-2100 NCT06245915); CLDN6 CAR-T ± RNA vaccine for CLDN6+ R/R GCT (BNT211-01, NCT04503278).
- Decision: *** SCD gene therapy center referral (Casgevy vs Lyfgenia) / heme CAR-T product if label criteria met / lifileucel if melanoma criteria met / investigational gene-cellular trial / disease-page next systemic line / supportive care.
- Labs/NGS: *** IgG, CBC with differential, disease markers/MRD as indicated; antigen/HLA testing if solid-tumor cellular trial considered.
- Referrals: *** cellular-therapy / CAR-T program, SCD gene-therapy center, fertility preservation, trial coordinator, disease-specific oncologist/hematologist.
- Follow-up: *** (include CRS/ICANS window; engraftment labs; IgG/IVIG plan; secondary malignancy surveillance — CAR-T lifelong; Lyfgenia CBC q6 mo ≥15 y; SCD organ monitoring).
