#GeneCellularOverview
- Counseling: Gene and cellular therapy is a separate clinic track from GU disease-setting SOC. FDA-approved autologous CAR-T products are labeled for defined hematologic cancers (CD19/BCMA class); lifileucel (Amtagvi) is approved TIL therapy for unresectable/metastatic melanoma after PD-1 (± BRAF pathway therapy if indicated). No FDA-approved CAR-T/TCR-T/TIL product is standard for prostate, urothelial, RCC, or testis cancers on this wiki.
- Counseling: Do not classify Pluvicto/radioligands, ADCs, or targeted small molecules as gene/cellular therapy — those stay on the disease page.
- Counseling: For GU solid tumors progressing after standard options, investigational referral examples include MAGE-A4 TCR-T (SURPASS / ADP-A2M4CD8, NCT04044859) if HLA-A*02 and MAGE-A4 criteria; PSMA/STEAP1 CAR-T trials in mCRPC (e.g. NCT03089203, NCT06236139); allogeneic CD70 CAR-T / logic-gated cells in ccRCC after ICI+VEGF (e.g. TRAVERSE/ALLO-316 NCT04696731; AB-2100 NCT06245915); CLDN6 CAR-T ± RNA vaccine for CLDN6+ R/R GCT (BNT211-01, NCT04503278). These are not SOC.
- Counseling: Reviewed CRS/ICANS and cytopenia/infection risks for cellular therapy and the need for protocol- or label-directed monitoring. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
