Who this applies to

PopulationExact POUT boundaryNoneligible boundary
Resected high-risk UTUCPredominantly transitional-cell UTUC after en-bloc radical nephroureterectomy; pT2–pT4, any N, M0 or pT any, N1–3, M0.Metastatic disease, residual lymphadenopathy when postoperative imaging was required, or disease outside these pathologic criteria.
Fit for postoperative platinumTreatment began within 90 days; satisfactory hematologic/biochemical profile; GFR ≥30 mL/min.Do not generalize the protocol to frail patients with preserved GFR but another cisplatin contraindication.
Protocol platinum selectionGFR ≥50: gemcitabine–cisplatin; GFR 30–49: gemcitabine–carboplatin.POUT was not designed to compare cisplatin with carboplatin.

Therapy sequencing

Postoperative stateNext treatment orderBranch point / evidence boundary
pT2–T4 and/or pN+ UTUC after RNU, no prior neoadjuvant platinumStart four cycles of adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum within the POUT postoperative window.This is the direct POUT sequence; it does not compare adjuvant chemotherapy with neoadjuvant chemotherapy.
Post-RNU GFR ≥50 mL/minGemcitabine–cisplatin.This is POUT protocol selection, not a universal cisplatin eligibility definition.

Platinum-ineligible or declining, high-risk diseaseDiscuss nivolumab or trial/surveillance with explicit UTUC evidence limits.CheckMate 274 is all-MIUC; do not claim an unreported UTUC subgroup efficacy result.
No prior bladder cancer after RNUDeliver the separate single intravesical postoperative chemotherapy intervention.This reduces bladder recurrence; it is not systemic recurrence treatment.

Surveillance and treatment de-escalation

After RNU (surveillance arm or post-chemo)CT chest/abdomen/pelvis every 6 months × 2 years, then annually per EAU UTUC frameworks; cystoscopy/urinary tract evaluation for bladder recurrence; single postoperative intravesical chemotherapy when indicated.Complete four cycles of adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum within 90 days if selected; then surveillance only.Local recurrence → re-resection when feasible; metastatic recurrence → systemic therapy (metastatic UTUC/mUC page).
On adjuvant gemcitabine–platinumCBC, renal function, electrolytes each cycle; audiometry if cisplatin.Stop after four cycles per POUT; hold for febrile neutropenia or renal decline.Progression on therapy → systemic second-line options; platinum-ineligible may discuss nivolumab with explicit all-MIUC evidence boundary.
  • Bladder recurrence surveillance is distinct from systemic UTUC recurrence monitoring.

Epic phrase

#utucadjuvant

  • Counseling: POUT adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum after RNU: 3-year disease-free survival about 71 in 100 vs 46 in 100 (HR 0.45); 5-year DFS about 62% vs 45% (HR 0.55). Five-year OS 66% vs 57%; adjusted OS HR 0.76 (CI crossed 1)—DFS is the established benefit. Nivolumab adjuvant data are from all-MIUC CheckMate 274, not a verified UTUC subgroup estimate.
  • Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: four adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum cycles then stop; CT every 6 months ×2 years then annually plus bladder-recurrence cystoscopy; single postoperative intravesical chemotherapy when indicated; metastatic recurrence → systemic pathway.
  • Counseling: Reviewed sequence: post-RNU → four cycles of gemcitabine–platinum; use cisplatin at POUT GFR ≥50 and carboplatin at GFR 30–49. Single intravesical chemotherapy is a separate bladder-recurrence intervention; nivolumab for platinum-ineligible disease requires an explicit all-MIUC evidence discussion. ***
  • Monitoring: With gemcitabine–platinum, check CBC, renal function, electrolytes, neuropathy/hearing, and nausea/vomiting. With nivolumab, review bowel, lung, liver, skin, and endocrine symptoms; after intravesical treatment, review fever, worsening urinary symptoms, dysuria, and hematuria. Contact oncology urgently for fever, infection symptoms, bleeding, uncontrolled vomiting, dehydration, or concerning immune symptoms. ***
  • Decision: *** selects adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum / surveillance / further recovery or trial assessment.
  • Labs/NGS: CBC, CMP, renal function *, pathology stage *, postoperative imaging ***.
  • Procedures: *** intravesical recurrence-prevention discussion and multidisciplinary review.
  • Follow-up: *** treatment monitoring, cystoscopic/bladder surveillance, and imaging.
