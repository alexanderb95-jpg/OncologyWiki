Evidence page

Who this applies to

  • mCRPC: radiographic and/or biochemical progression on ADT with castrate testosterone.
  • Document de novo vs recurrent HSPC, volume, visceral disease, and the full prior-therapy list (ARPI, docetaxel in HSPC vs CRPC, RLT, PARP). Prior HSPC Pluvicto (PSMAddition) changes marrow/eligibility counseling.
  • Fitness for taxane, marrow reserve (for RLT or chemo), renal function, dental status, and whether visceral or bulky nodal disease excludes Ra-223.

  • Continue ADT in all patients.
  • ARPI-naive: abiraterone/prednisone or enzalutamide. If BRCA (and selected HRR per current FDA label) and ARPI-naive: PARP + ARPI combination — US labels are biomarker-restricted; do not use all-comer PROpel/TALAPRO-2 analyses as the indication.
  • After prior ARPI: if PSMA+, Pluvicto (VISION post-taxane; PSMAfore taxane-naive vs ARPI switch). Fit chemo-naive: docetaxel (TAX327). After ARPI + docetaxel: Pluvicto (PSMA+) or cabazitaxel (CARD preferred over ARPI switch).
  • BRCA / selected HRR after ARPI: PARP monotherapy (PROfound olaparib; TRITON3 rucaparib — benefit concentrated in BRCA; ATM exploratory HR 0.95 in TRITON3).
  • Symptomatic bone-predominant, no visceral disease: radium-223 (ALSYMPCA). PEACE-3: enzalutamide + Ra-223 improved rPFS and OS vs enza alone; co-administer a bone-protecting agent. Do not pair Ra-223 with abiraterone (that combination was not in the retrieved ALSYMPCA or PEACE-3 sources).
  • MSI-H/dMMR: pembrolizumab (later-line, histology-agnostic) — prostate-specific RCT numbers not in this pack.
  • CONTACT-02 (cabozantinib + atezolizumab vs ARPI switch) improved PFS not OS — not a default OS-proven SOC.
  • Clinical trial and best supportive care at every decision point.

Biomarkers

  • PSMA PET (approved tracer) for Pluvicto eligibility; mixed/PSMA-negative lesions argue against RLT.
  • Germline + somatic NGS for BRCA1/2 and other HRR genes; use the companion diagnostic that matches the intended PARP. ATM ≠ BRCA (TRITON3).
  • MSI / dMMR / TMB for pembrolizumab.
  • Testosterone to confirm castration.

Upcoming trial results

  • Earlier Pluvicto (PSMAddition already moved RLT into PSMA+ HSPC).
  • CONTACT-02: PFS without OS — not a new default.
  • AR degraders / novel AR ligands; lineage plasticity / NEPC.
  • Optimal sequence among Pluvicto, taxane, and PARP after ARPI remains unsettled (no head-to-head in this pack).

Epic phrase

#mCRPC

  • Counseling: Reviewed treatment sequencing with continued ADT, prior ARPI/taxane exposure, PSMA imaging, marrow reserve, and molecular testing. Reviewed taxane, radioligand, PARP, radium-223, and ARPI-specific toxicities.
  • Monitoring: Continue ADT and monitor blood pressure, potassium/liver testing with abiraterone, and falls/cognition/cardiovascular and bone effects with ARPIs. Check CBC for taxanes, PARP therapy, radium-223, and Pluvicto; check renal function with Pluvicto; use bone protection with radium-223; review bowel/lung/liver/endocrine symptoms with pembrolizumab. Contact oncology promptly for fever/infection, progressive neuropathy, severe diarrhea/dehydration, marked fatigue, dyspnea, bleeding, new fracture symptoms, or other concerning treatment effects. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
  • Discussed progression to mCRPC in the setting of * (PSA / radiographic / symptomatic) on *, with castrate testosterone. Prior therapies: * (ADT ± ARPI ± docetaxel in HSPC; any CRPC lines; *prior Pluvicto in HSPC)
