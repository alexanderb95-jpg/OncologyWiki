  • Confirm castrate testosterone, PSA kinetics, conventional imaging, prior androgen-receptor pathway inhibitor exposure, falls/fracture risk, blood pressure, cognition, cardiovascular comorbidity, and patient priorities before selecting treatment1011.

Landmark evidence

The phase 3 studies were placebo-controlled, conventional-imaging nmCRPC trials. The final OS time points differ—median OS in PROSPER and SPARTAN versus 3-year OS in ARAMIS—and must not be compared across trials123456.

Biomarkers

Marker or featureRole nowPractical boundary
PSA doubling timeTrial risk-enrichment and eligibility factor135A PSADT ≤10 months defined the pivotal-trial population; it is not an agent-selecting biomarker135.
Conventional imagingDisease-state classification boundary135The phase 3 evidence used conventional CT/MRI and bone imaging to define nonmetastatic disease135.
Molecular testingNo validated treatment selector135The pivotal eligibility texts did not report molecular enrollment criteria that select among the three agents135.
PSMA PETStaging information, not a validated treatment selector7In one retrospective 200-patient high-risk cohort, PSMA PET found M1 disease in 55% despite negative conventional imaging; prospective treatment-guidance value remains unestablished. Fendler et al.7

Upcoming research

Field direction: PSMA-directed approaches in conventional-imaging M0 / PET-positive CRPC and real-world ARPI delivery data may refine nmCRPC counseling, but do not yet replace the three ARPI phase 3 standards8910.

No new practice-changing randomized result was identified in the source pack10.

Patient counseling and action plan

  • Contact oncology promptly for a new extensive rash, marked fatigue, a fall or suspected fracture, marked dizziness, new confusion or memory change, palpitations, chest symptoms, or another concerning treatment effect. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed135.
  • Maintain fall and fracture-risk assessment, blood-pressure monitoring, and thyroid assessment where clinically indicated; treatment holds or discontinuation may be required135.

Epic phrase

#nmcrpc

  • Counseling: Reviewed high-risk nmCRPC as defined in the pivotal trials: castrate testosterone, no metastases on conventional imaging, and PSA doubling time ≤10 months. PSMA-PET-only disease and PSA doubling time >10 months did not have direct pivotal-trial efficacy estimates.
  • Counseling: In PROSPER, metastasis-free survival was about 37 vs 15 months with placebo (HR 0.29 vs placebo); final median overall survival 67.0 vs 56.3 months with placebo (HR 0.73 vs placebo). SPARTAN apalutamide MFS 40.5 vs 16.2 months with placebo (HR 0.28 vs placebo); final median OS 73.9 vs 59.9 months with placebo (HR 0.78 vs placebo). ARAMIS darolutamide MFS 40.4 vs 18.4 months with placebo (HR 0.41 vs placebo); 3-year OS about 83% vs 77% with placebo (HR 0.69 vs placebo). Trials do not rank agents head-to-head.
  • Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: PSA every 3 months with conventional imaging every 6–12 months; continue ADT plus ARPI until conventional-imaging metastasis or toxicity; PSMA-PET-only disease lacks pivotal efficacy estimates; conventional metastasis → mCRPC pathway.
  • Counseling: Reviewed continued ADT plus apalutamide, enzalutamide, or darolutamide as unranked options. Discussed that each placebo-controlled trial improved metastasis-free survival and final overall-survival measures within its own trial; no head-to-head comparison ranks agents.
  • Counseling: Reviewed sequence: continue ADT → add one ARPI for high-risk conventional-imaging nmCRPC; at conventional-imaging metastatic progression, continue ADT and move to mCRPC sequencing with prior ARPI exposure documented. ***
  • Counseling: Reviewed treatment-specific safety. Enzalutamide counseling includes fatigue, hypertension, dizziness, falls, and fracture risk; apalutamide counseling includes fatigue, hypertension, rash, falls, fractures, and hypothyroidism; darolutamide counseling includes fatigue and hypertension. Treatment interruption or discontinuation may be needed.
  • Counseling: Promptly contact oncology for an extensive rash, marked fatigue, marked dizziness, a fall or suspected fracture, new confusion or memory change, palpitations, chest symptoms, or another concerning treatment effect. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
  • Decision: Continue ADT. *** selects / defers apalutamide / enzalutamide / darolutamide / surveillance / clinical-trial evaluation after shared decision-making.
  • Labs/NGS: Confirmed castrate testosterone *; PSA doubling time *; conventional imaging ***. Monitor blood pressure; obtain thyroid testing when clinically indicated. Molecular testing does not select among these agents.
  • Procedures: *** PSMA PET / conventional imaging surveillance / fall and bone-health assessment planned.
  • Medications: *** continue ADT and start / defer androgen-receptor pathway inhibitor.
  • Referrals: *** endocrinology / cardiology / geriatric or physical-therapy fall assessment / clinical-trial consultation, as indicated.
  • Follow-up: *** PSA, testosterone, toxicity, blood-pressure, fall-risk, and imaging follow-up interval.
