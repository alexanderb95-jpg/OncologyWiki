Therapy sequencing

Starting pointPreferred orderBranch point / evidence boundary
Cisplatin-eligible, resectable MIBCNeoadjuvant durvalumab plus GC ×4 → cystectomy → adjuvant durvalumab ×8, or perioperative EV plus pembrolizumab → cystectomy → postoperative EV plus pembrolizumab.NIAGARA and EV-304 are distinct perioperative strategies; their results do not establish a sequence of one regimen after the other.
Cisplatin-ineligible or declining cisplatinEV plus pembrolizumab ×3 → cystectomy → postoperative EV plus pembrolizumab.EV-303 directly studied this population and this full perioperative sequence.
New obstruction or borderline renal functionRelieve obstruction when feasible → restage renal function and cisplatin eligibility → choose the perioperative pathway.This is a pretreatment decision step, not evidence that a pre-drainage creatinine permanently determines eligibility.
Not a cystectomy candidate or declining cystectomyRefer to a bladder-preservation pathway rather than applying perioperative-trial efficacy estimates.The listed phase 3 regimens are cystectomy-anchored and do not establish a bladder-preservation sequence.

Epic phrase

#neoadjuvantmibc

  • Counseling: Reviewed resectable muscle-invasive bladder cancer, cystectomy anchoring, and perioperative options based on cisplatin eligibility, pathology, renal function after obstruction relief, neuropathy, hearing, performance status, and patient priorities.
  • Counseling: Reviewed treatment order: cisplatin-eligible patients may receive perioperative durvalumab plus GC or perioperative EV plus pembrolizumab → cystectomy → the regimen's specified postoperative treatment; cisplatin-ineligible/declining patients have EV-303 support for EV plus pembrolizumab → cystectomy → postoperative EV plus pembrolizumab. These are distinct perioperative strategies, not sequential regimens. ***
  • Monitoring: For EV plus pembrolizumab, review numbness/weakness, rash, vision changes, thirst/polyuria, creatinine, and immune symptoms before each dose. The FDA label reports EV-303 neuropathy 39%, rash 54%, and increased glucose 72%; EV-304 neuropathy 43%, rash 63%, and hyperglycemia 20%. For durvalumab plus GC, monitor CBC, renal function, electrolytes, neuropathy/hearing, and immune symptoms. Promptly report rash, numbness/weakness, vision changes, thirst/polyuria, cough/dyspnea, diarrhea, fever, or other concerning treatment effects. ***
  • Decision: *** selects perioperative EV plus pembrolizumab / perioperative durvalumab plus GC / another cystectomy-anchored plan / bladder-preservation evaluation.
  • Labs/NGS: CBC, CMP, renal function, glucose, baseline neuropathy/hearing assessment ***.
  • Procedures: *** urinary obstruction relief, restaging, cystectomy/PLND planning, and multidisciplinary review.
  • Follow-up: *** toxicity review, surgical timing, and postoperative treatment planning.
