Evidence page

Who this applies to

  • Unresectable locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma1011.
  • Record cisplatin eligibility, prior perioperative/adjuvant IO or EV, sites (liver), neuropathy, glycemic status, FGFR3, HER2 IHC, PD-L1 (less central for EV+pembro)17810.

Subsequent therapyEV, biomarker-directed therapy, trial, or supportive care4510Match prior therapy and FGFR/HER2 status58.Regimen-specific78.
  • 1L most patients: EV 1.25 mg/kg d1,8 + pembrolizumab 200 mg d1 q3wk (EV-302)1.
  • 1L cisplatin-eligible if not using EV+pembro: nivo 360 mg + GC q3wk ×6 then nivo 480 mg q4wk (CheckMate 901)2.
  • 1L platinum then, if no PD: maintenance avelumab (JAVELIN)3. This pathway is largely displaced by EV+pembro but still applies if platinum was already given13.
  • Post platinum + PD-(L)1: EV monotherapy (EV-301)4.
  • FGFR3-altered after ≥1 line including PD-(L)1: erdafitinib (THOR cohort 1)5. Do not use erdafitinib in PD-(L)1-naive eligible patients (FDA limitation; THOR cohort 2 OS HR 1.18 vs pembro)8.
  • HER2-expressing: discuss T-DXd / other HER2 ADCs as trial or label-permitted tumor-agnostic options; RC48-C016 is not a verified US SOC in this pack1011.
  • After platinum, pembrolizumab vs chemo (KEYNOTE-045) if no prior PD-11011.
  • Supportive care / trial1011.

Biomarkers

  • FGFR3 (companion diagnostic) for erdafitinib after PD-(L)158.
  • HER2 IHC: RC48-C016 used 1+/2+/3+; T-DXd greatest benefit in IHC 3+ across tumors (DESTINY-PanTumor02) — bladder-only numbers not in sources1011.
  • PD-L1: not the selector for EV+pembro 1L in EV-302 as reported here1.
  • NGS for FGFR and other trial targets510.

Upcoming trial results

  • Sequencing after 1L EV+pembro (FGFR, HER2, taxane, SG) — phase 3 post-EV+pembro data not in this pack110.
  • US regulatory status of disitamab vedotin + toripalimab1011.
  • T-DXd urothelial-specific efficacy (not in retrieved DESTINY abstract)1011.

Epic phrase

#Metastatic urothelial carcinoma

  • Counseling: First-line EV plus pembrolizumab (EV-302) improved median overall survival about 32 vs 16 months with platinum chemotherapy (OS HR 0.47 vs platinum). CheckMate 901 nivolumab plus GC median OS 21.7 vs 18.9 months with GC alone (HR 0.78 vs GC). Post-platinum maintenance avelumab median OS 21.4 vs 14.3 months with BSC alone (HR 0.69 vs BSC). After platinum plus PD-(L)1, EV monotherapy median OS 12.9 vs 9.0 months with chemotherapy (HR 0.70 vs chemo). FGFR-altered after PD-(L)1: erdafitinib median OS 12.1 vs 7.8 months with chemotherapy (HR 0.64 vs chemo).
  • Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: imaging every 9–12 weeks on active therapy; hold EV for neuropathy ≥2 or hyperglycemia; continue avelumab maintenance until progression or 2-year cap; progression after EV+pembro → biomarker-directed or trial options.
  • Monitoring: EV plus pembrolizumab requires review of new or worsening numbness/weakness, rash, vision changes, and thirst/polyuria before each dose; the EV-302 label reports treatment-arm rates (single-arm label rates; no paired comparator % in the retained label table): peripheral neuropathy 67%, rash 68%, and increased glucose 66%. Trial-level grade ≥3 TRAEs were 55.9% vs 69.5% with platinum chemotherapy. EV monotherapy requires the same surveillance, with EV-301 treatment-arm rates: peripheral neuropathy 48.0%, hyperglycemia 6.8%, and skin reactions 47.3%. Erdafitinib requires phosphate and scheduled ocular monitoring; sacituzumab requires CBC and diarrhea review.
  • Counseling: Promptly contact oncology for progressive numbness/weakness, rash, visual symptoms, thirst/polyuria, cough/dyspnea, diarrhea, fever/infection, or other concerning treatment effects. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
