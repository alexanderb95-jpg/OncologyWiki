Evidence page

Who this applies to

  • Unresectable locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma.
  • Record cisplatin eligibility, prior perioperative/adjuvant IO or EV, sites (liver), neuropathy, glycemic status, FGFR3, HER2 IHC, PD-L1 (less central for EV+pembro).

Standard options

Clinical contextOptionKey inclusionTypical course
Untreated la/mUCEV plus pembrolizumabAssess neuropathy, glucose, organ function, and prior perioperative exposure.EV days 1 and 8 plus pembrolizumab every 3 weeks in EV-302.
Cisplatin-eligible untreated la/mUC not using EV plus pembrolizumabNivolumab plus GCCheckMate 901-type cisplatin fitness.GC induction then nivolumab maintenance per trial.
No progression after platinumAvelumab maintenanceJAVELIN Bladder 100 population.Continue until progression or unacceptable toxicity.
Subsequent therapyEV, biomarker-directed therapy, trial, or supportive careMatch prior therapy and FGFR/HER2 status.Regimen-specific.
  • 1L most patients: EV 1.25 mg/kg d1,8 + pembrolizumab 200 mg d1 q3wk (EV-302).
  • 1L cisplatin-eligible if not using EV+pembro: nivo 360 mg + GC q3wk ×6 then nivo 480 mg q4wk (CheckMate 901).
  • 1L platinum then, if no PD: maintenance avelumab (JAVELIN). This pathway is largely displaced by EV+pembro but still applies if platinum was already given.
  • Post platinum + PD-(L)1: EV monotherapy (EV-301).
  • FGFR3-altered after ≥1 line including PD-(L)1: erdafitinib (THOR cohort 1). Do not use erdafitinib in PD-(L)1-naive eligible patients (FDA limitation; THOR cohort 2 OS HR 1.18 vs pembro).
  • HER2-expressing: discuss T-DXd / other HER2 ADCs as trial or label-permitted tumor-agnostic options; RC48-C016 is not a verified US SOC in this pack.
  • After platinum, pembrolizumab vs chemo (KEYNOTE-045) if no prior PD-1.
  • Supportive care / trial.

Biomarkers

  • FGFR3 (companion diagnostic) for erdafitinib after PD-(L)1.
  • HER2 IHC: RC48-C016 used 1+/2+/3+; T-DXd greatest benefit in IHC 3+ across tumors (DESTINY-PanTumor02) — bladder-only numbers not in sources.
  • PD-L1: not the selector for EV+pembro 1L in EV-302 as reported here.
  • NGS for FGFR and other trial targets.

Upcoming trial results

  • Sequencing after 1L EV+pembro (FGFR, HER2, taxane, SG) — phase 3 post-EV+pembro data not in this pack.
  • US regulatory status of disitamab vedotin + toripalimab.
  • T-DXd urothelial-specific efficacy (not in retrieved DESTINY abstract).

Epic phrase

#Metastatic urothelial carcinoma

  • Counseling: Reviewed systemic-treatment selection by prior perioperative exposure, platinum eligibility, neuropathy/glycemic status, FGFR/HER2 testing, symptoms, and organ function.
  • Monitoring: EV plus pembrolizumab requires review of new or worsening numbness/weakness, rash, vision changes, and thirst/polyuria before each dose; the EV-302 label reports peripheral neuropathy 67%, rash 68%, and increased glucose 66%. EV monotherapy requires the same surveillance, with EV-301 peripheral neuropathy 48.0%, hyperglycemia 6.8%, and skin reactions 47.3%. Erdafitinib requires phosphate and scheduled ocular monitoring; sacituzumab requires CBC and diarrhea review.
  • Counseling: Promptly contact oncology for progressive numbness/weakness, rash, visual symptoms, thirst/polyuria, cough/dyspnea, diarrhea, fever/infection, or other concerning treatment effects. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
