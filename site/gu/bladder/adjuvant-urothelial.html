Evidence page

Who this applies to

  • Post-cystectomy (or appropriately selected post-nephroureterectomy) with high-risk pathologic features (CheckMate 274: ypT2–T4a and/or ypN+ after neoadjuvant cisplatin, or pT3–T4a and/or pN+ without neoadjuvant chemo — exact eligibility in the protocol/label; restage from source if needed).
  • Note neoadjuvant / perioperative exposure: prior EV+pembro or durvalumab changes adjuvant counseling (overlap, cumulative neuropathy, irAE).
  • Cisplatin eligibility, residual disease, margins, histology/variant, surgical recovery, and ctDNA MRD status if considering IMvigor011-path atezolizumab.

  • If perioperative EV+pembro or NIAGARA already given or planned as the MIBC pathway: adjuvant counseling is part of that perioperative package, not a separate CheckMate 274 default.
  • If high-risk after RC and no prior perioperative ICI: adjuvant nivolumab 240 mg q2wk ×1 year (CheckMate 274) vs observation vs trial. AMBASSADOR pembrolizumab 200 mg q3wk ×1 year is DFS-positive; OS not in the retrieved abstract.
  • If post-cystectomy ctDNA MRD+ by Signatera CDx (serial testing window per label): adjuvant atezolizumab (IMvigor011 / FDA 15 May 2026) — distinct from the negative unselected IMvigor010 trial.
  • Do not use adjuvant atezolizumab based on IMvigor010 (unselected high-risk).
  • Observation remains reasonable when risk is lower, recovery is poor, ctDNA remains negative, or irAE risk is prohibitive.
  • Upper-tract: CheckMate 274 enrolled MIUC including UTUC; do not invent UTUC-specific HRs — not separately reported in the retrieved abstracts.

Biomarkers

  • PD-L1 is not required for the CheckMate 274 ITT DFS result; PD-L1 ≥1% subgroup had a larger DFS HR (0.55 primary; 0.58 at 5 y). Do not withhold nivo solely for PD-L1-negative disease based on ITT.
  • ctDNA MRD (Signatera CDx): selects adjuvant atezolizumab after cystectomy (IMvigor011 / FDA). Serial testing until positive or end of recommended 12-month window per label. CheckMate 274 5-year exploratory ctDNA remains exploratory — not the registration companion for nivo.
  • Nectin-4 / HER2 / FGFR do not currently select adjuvant IO in these trials.

Epic phrase

#Adjuvant urothelial carcinoma

  • Counseling: CheckMate 274 adjuvant nivolumab improved disease-free survival versus observation in high-risk MIUC (DFS HR 0.70). AMBASSADOR pembrolizumab improved DFS (HR 0.73); overall survival was not reported in the retrieved abstract. IMvigor011 ctDNA MRD-positive adjuvant atezolizumab improved DFS (HR 0.64) and OS (HR 0.59)—distinct from negative unselected IMvigor010. Prior perioperative EV/pembro or NIAGARA changes who still needs separate adjuvant ICI.
  • Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: CT every 3–6 months ×2 years then every 6–12 months; adjuvant PD-1 for up to 1 year then stop unless progression/toxicity; ctDNA-negative patients should not receive unselected adjuvant atezolizumab; recurrence → metastatic urothelial pathway.
  • Monitoring: With adjuvant nivolumab, pembrolizumab, or atezolizumab, review new diarrhea/abdominal pain, cough/dyspnea, jaundice, rash, marked fatigue, palpitations, or temperature/weight intolerance before every treatment. With perioperative EV plus pembrolizumab, additionally review numbness/weakness, vision change, thirst/polyuria, and rash; with durvalumab plus GC, monitor CBC, renal function, electrolytes, neuropathy/hearing, and immune symptoms. Promptly contact oncology for these symptoms. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
  • Discussed pathology and staging: *** (ypT / pT, N, margins, histology, variant features).
  • Discussed perioperative course: neoadjuvant / perioperative * (EV+pembro vs durvalumab+GC vs GC vs none; cycles / response); surgery date *. Discussed that perioperative EV+pembrolizumab is FDA-approved for MIBC cystectomy candidates regardless of cisplatin eligibility (EV-303 cisplatin-ineligible; EV-304 cisplatin-eligible, FDA 10 Jul 2026) and NIAGARA (durvalumab + GC) remains an alternative for cisplatin-eligible patients — these change adjuvant counseling. Adjuvant nivolumab (CheckMate 274) is for high-risk patients who did not already receive perioperative ICI. For post-cystectomy ctDNA MRD+ (Signatera CDx), adjuvant atezolizumab (IMvigor011) is FDA-approved — distinct from negative unselected IMvigor010.
