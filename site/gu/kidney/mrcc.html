Evidence page

Who this applies to

  • Metastatic RCC; record histology (clear vs non-clear), sarcomatoid, IMDC risk (KPS, time from diagnosis to systemic therapy, hemoglobin, calcium, neutrophils, platelets), prior nephrectomy, prior adjuvant PD-1.

Standard options

Non-clear-cell or atypical clinical contextTrial or individualized systemic approachDo not apply clear-cell trial effect estimates unchanged.Individualized.
  • IMDC intermediate/poor, goal durability / CR: nivo 3 mg/kg + ipi 1 mg/kg q3wk ×4 then nivo (CheckMate 214). Higher-grade irAE than TKI monotherapy; lower grade 3–4 TRAE than sunitinib in the primary paper.
  • Any IMDC, need response / TKI partner acceptable: pembro + axitinib (KEYNOTE-426), nivo + cabo 40 mg (CheckMate 9ER), or pembro + lenvatinib 20 mg (CLEAR). Hypertension, diarrhea, hand-foot, hepatic toxicity.
  • Do not add cabo to nivo/ipi as default (COSMIC-313: no OS gain, more grade 3/4 TRAE).
  • Later line after VEGFR and ICI: belzutifan vs everolimus (LITESPARK-005) improved PFS/ORR not OS. Nivo vs everolimus (CheckMate 025) is historical post-VEGFR. TiNivo-2: adding nivo to tivozanib after ICI did not improve PFS.
  • Local therapy (CN, MDT) is individualized — not covered by these systemic trials.

Biomarkers

  • Histology and IMDC risk are the practical selectors. PD-L1 is not the primary selector for these doublets.
  • KEYNOTE-426 5-year: TcellinfGEP and angiogenesis signatures were associated with outcomes (exploratory).
  • Occasional germline (VHL, FH, etc.) for non-clear / hereditary syndromes — not required to start 1L IO doublets in sporadic ccRCC.

Upcoming trial results

  • Adjuvant pembro exposure at relapse (KEYNOTE-564).
  • HIF-2 (belzutifan) moving earlier; combinations not in this 1L pack.
  • Non-clear-cell randomized 1L data — not retrieved as a dedicated landscape here.
  • No OS benefit from COSMIC-313 triplet.

Epic phrase

#Metastatic renal cell carcinoma

  • Counseling: CheckMate 214 nivolumab/ipilimumab in IMDC intermediate/poor risk: 18-month OS about 75% vs 60% (OS HR 0.63). KEYNOTE-426 pembrolizumab/axitinib 12-month OS about 90% vs 78% (interim OS HR 0.53). CheckMate 9ER nivo/cabo PFS HR 0.51. No head-to-head comparison ranks doublets. Later belzutifan improved PFS after prior ICI and VEGFR therapy; OS was not statistically significant at reported analysis.
  • Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: imaging every 8–12 weeks on first-line therapy; complete nivo/ipi induction then maintenance; hold TKI/IO for toxicity; progression after ICI and VEGFR → belzutifan or trial; treatment breaks for doublets are not trial-established.
  • Monitoring: For IO/IO, review bowel, lung, liver, skin, and endocrine symptoms before each infusion. For IO/TKI, also check blood pressure, urine protein, liver tests, diarrhea, and hand-foot/skin effects. For belzutifan, check CBC and oxygen saturation. Promptly contact oncology for diarrhea/abdominal pain, cough/dyspnea, rash, jaundice, marked fatigue, hypertension symptoms, hand-foot/skin changes, or anemia/hypoxia symptoms. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.
