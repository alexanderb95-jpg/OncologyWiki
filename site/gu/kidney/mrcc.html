Evidence page

Who this applies to

  • Metastatic RCC; record histology (clear vs non-clear), sarcomatoid, IMDC risk (KPS, time from diagnosis to systemic therapy, hemoglobin, calcium, neutrophils, platelets), prior nephrectomy, prior adjuvant PD-167.

Standard options

Clinical contextOptionKey inclusionTypical course
IMDC intermediate/poor risk, durability/complete-response priorityNivolumab plus ipilimumab1Clear-cell advanced RCC and immune-therapy candidacy1.Induction followed by nivolumab maintenance in CheckMate 2141.
Any IMDC risk with need for response and an acceptable TKI partnerPD-1/VEGFR combination234Balance disease burden, organ function, hypertension, and toxicity profile67.Regimen-specific continuous TKI plus PD-1 schedule234.
After ICI and antiangiogenic therapyBelzutifan or other sequence matched to prior exposure5LITESPARK-005 population and local label5.Regimen-specific5.
Non-clear-cell or atypical clinical contextTrial or individualized systemic approach67Do not apply clear-cell trial effect estimates unchanged67.Individualized6.
  • IMDC intermediate/poor, goal durability / CR: nivo 3 mg/kg + ipi 1 mg/kg q3wk ×4 then nivo (CheckMate 214)1. Higher-grade irAE than TKI monotherapy; lower grade 3–4 TRAE than sunitinib in the primary paper1.
  • Any IMDC, need response / TKI partner acceptable: pembro + axitinib (KEYNOTE-426)2, nivo + cabo 40 mg (CheckMate 9ER)3, or pembro + lenvatinib 20 mg (CLEAR)4. Hypertension, diarrhea, hand-foot, hepatic toxicity234.
  • Do not add cabo to nivo/ipi as default (COSMIC-313: no OS gain, more grade 3/4 TRAE)67.
  • Later line after VEGFR and ICI: belzutifan vs everolimus (LITESPARK-005) improved PFS/ORR not OS5. Nivo vs everolimus (CheckMate 025) is historical post-VEGFR6. TiNivo-2: adding nivo to tivozanib after ICI did not improve PFS67.
  • Local therapy (CN, MDT) is individualized — not covered by these systemic trials67.

Gene and cellular therapy

No FDA-approved CAR-T, TCR-T, TIL, or gene-edited cell product is standard for metastatic RCC on this page67. IO/TKI doublets, belzutifan, and other small molecules are not gene or cellular therapy15.

Upcoming research

Field direction: first-line IO doublets remain the core; HIF-2 (belzutifan) is moving earlier and into combinations, adjuvant pembrolizumab exposure changes relapse counseling, and non-clear-cell randomized first-line data remain thinner than clear-cell567.

Epic phrase

#Metastatic renal cell carcinoma

  • Counseling: CheckMate 214 nivolumab/ipilimumab in IMDC intermediate/poor risk: 18-month OS about 75% vs 60% with sunitinib (OS HR 0.63 vs sunitinib). KEYNOTE-426 pembrolizumab/axitinib 12-month OS about 90% vs 78% with sunitinib (interim OS HR 0.53 vs sunitinib). CheckMate 9ER nivo/cabo PFS HR 0.51 vs sunitinib. No head-to-head comparison ranks doublets. Later belzutifan improved PFS vs everolimus after prior ICI and VEGFR therapy; OS was not statistically significant at reported analysis.
  • Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: imaging every 8–12 weeks during active response assessment on first-line therapy; after prolonged response, extend imaging intervals per EAU/NCCN when clinically appropriate. Complete nivo/ipi induction then maintenance; hold TKI/IO for toxicity; progression after ICI and VEGFR → belzutifan or trial; treatment breaks for doublets are not trial-established.
  • Monitoring: For IO/IO, review bowel, lung, liver, skin, and endocrine symptoms before each infusion. For IO/TKI, also check blood pressure, urine protein, liver tests, diarrhea, and hand-foot/skin effects. For belzutifan, check CBC and oxygen saturation. Promptly contact oncology for diarrhea/abdominal pain, cough/dyspnea, rash, jaundice, marked fatigue, hypertension symptoms, hand-foot/skin changes, or anemia/hypoxia symptoms. *** after-hours/urgent-assessment instructions reviewed.

  • Discussed treatment order: use IO/IO or an IO/TKI doublet first line according to risk, tumor burden, comorbidities, and priorities.
