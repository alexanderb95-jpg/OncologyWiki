Epic phrase

#metastaticgct

  • Counseling: Good-prognosis BEP ×3 vs BEP ×4: projected 2-year PFS about 90% vs 89%. IGCCCG update: 5-year OS about 96%/89%/67% for good/intermediate/poor-risk NSGCT (prognostic cohort; no randomized comparator). GETUG-13 intensification for selected poor-risk unfavorable marker decline: 5-year PFS about 59% vs 47% with continued BEP (HR 0.65 vs continued BEP); 5-year OS 71% vs 61% with continued BEP (HR 0.74 vs continued BEP, CI crossed 1). Treatment at an experienced germ-cell center is essential.
  • Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: markers each cycle during BEP/EP/VIP; complete planned cycles then stop (no first-line maintenance). After good/intermediate-prognosis complete remission, EAU-style follow-up: markers about 4×/year years 1–2 then 2×/year years 3–5; chest X-ray 1–2× year 1 then yearly; abdominopelvic MRI/CT 1–2× year 1 then at 24, 36, and 60 months; thorax CT if lung mets at diagnosis as indicated. Poor-prognosis or incomplete remission needs individualized specialist follow-up. Residual-mass PET (seminoma) or RPLND (NSGCT) per guideline; relapse → salvage at an expert center.
  • Counseling: Reviewed sequence: use BEP ×3 for good-risk disease, EP ×4 when bleomycin is contraindicated, BEP ×4 for intermediate/poor-risk disease, VIP ×4 with primary G-CSF when bleomycin cannot be used, and consider marker-directed intensification only for the selected poor-risk unfavorable-decline population. ***
  • Monitoring: Check CBC, renal function, hearing, neuropathy, nausea/vomiting, and fertility plan with all cisplatin/etoposide regimens; monitor pulmonary symptoms with bleomycin, hemorrhagic-cystitis symptoms with ifosfamide, and infection risk with VIP/intensification. Contact oncology urgently for fever, new dyspnea, worsening neuropathy, dehydration, hematuria, confusion, or uncontrolled vomiting. ***
  • Decision: *** selects BEP / EP / VIP / high-volume germ-cell center referral / trial consideration.
  • Labs/NGS: AFP, hCG, LDH, CBC, CMP, renal function *; baseline audiology/pulmonary evaluation *.
  • Procedures: *** sperm banking, central access, post-chemotherapy residual-mass planning.
  • Follow-up: *** markers each cycle, toxicity, response imaging, and survivorship plan.
