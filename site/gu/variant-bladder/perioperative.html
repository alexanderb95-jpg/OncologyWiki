Therapy sequencing

Variant / treatment statePreferred orderEvidence boundary
Resectable small-cell/neuroendocrine urothelial carcinomaNeuroendocrine-directed platinum-based systemic therapy → cystectomy or other local consolidation12.The subtype-specific evidence is phase 2 or retrospective; it does not prove one platinum regimen against GC or ddMVAC12.
Other aggressive variants or extensive differentiationExpert GU pathology review → subtype-aware, cystectomy-anchored multidisciplinary plan93.Do not substitute general-MIBC perioperative results for variant-specific proof without a reported subtype analysis93.
High-risk disease after cystectomy without neoadjuvant therapyConsider adjuvant cisplatin combination if eligible, or nivolumab when platinum is unsuitable56.The evidence base is primarily conventional urothelial carcinoma; do not serially add adjuvant therapy after untested perioperative combinations56.

Epic phrase

#variantbladderperiop

  • Counseling: Small-cell/neuroendocrine MIBC retrospective data: neoadjuvant chemotherapy before cystectomy associated with downstaging to ≤pT1N0 in about 62% vs 9% and longer median OS (159.5 vs 18.3 months)—retrospective, not randomized. Phase 2 small-cell regimen: 14/18 resectable patients downstaged; cT2N0 5-year OS about 80%. Variant meta-analysis pooled OS HR 0.74 for neoadjuvant chemotherapy—observational. Expert pathology review required.
  • Counseling: Reviewed surveillance and de-escalation: complete planned neoadjuvant cycles when feasible then local consolidation; post-cystectomy imaging per MIBC frameworks; adjuvant cisplatin or nivolumab only if no neoadjuvant given and conventional-MIUC criteria apply.
  • Counseling: For small-cell/neuroendocrine disease, reviewed preoperative platinum-based systemic therapy → local consolidation. For other variants, reviewed pathology-directed cystectomy planning; adjuvant cisplatin or nivolumab is considered only after cystectomy without neoadjuvant therapy when appropriate. ***
  • Monitoring: With platinum-based therapy, check CBC, renal function, neuropathy/hearing, electrolytes, and nausea/vomiting; with nivolumab, review bowel, lung, liver, skin, and endocrine symptoms. Promptly report fever, infection symptoms, bleeding, severe nausea/vomiting, dyspnea, diarrhea, rash, or endocrine symptoms. ***
  • Decision: *** selects pathology confirmation / systemic-therapy and cystectomy planning / another individualized approach / clinical-trial evaluation.
  • Labs/NGS: CBC, CMP, renal function, pathology review *, molecular testing *.
  • Procedures: *** cystectomy/PLND and multidisciplinary review.
  • Follow-up: *** toxicity, response, surgical timing, and survivorship plan.
