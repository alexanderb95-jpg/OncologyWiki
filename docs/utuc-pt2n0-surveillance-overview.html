pT2N0 UTUC after RNU — surveillance & recurrence over time

Muscle-invasive, node-negative upper tract urothelial carcinoma after radical nephroureterectomy: high-risk follow-up schedule, POUT recurrence landmarks, and adjuvant counseling context.

1. One-line answer

After RNU for pT2N0 UTUC, treat as high-risk: discuss adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum (POUT) within 90 days if fit, then follow EAU high-risk cystoscopy/cytology + CTU/chest CT every 6 months × 2 years → yearly. Published KM curves are from POUT’s mixed high-risk cohort (not a standalone pT2N0 curve); on surveillance, 3-year DFS ~46%, 5-year DFS ~45%, median DFS ~30 months.

2. Clinical framing

  • pT2N0 after radical nephroureterectomy = muscle-invasive, node-negative UTUC → EAU high-risk follow-up, not low-risk.
  • POUT-eligible (pT2–T4 Nany or N+ M0). Adjuvant chemo is the evidence-based default discussion if platinum-fit; surveillance alone is the POUT control arm.
  • Separate track: single postoperative intravesical chemotherapy (if no prior bladder cancer) to reduce bladder recurrence—not systemic DFS.

Why front-loaded imaging Most lung metastases after RNU occur within ~2 years; bladder and other-site risk falls substantially after ~4 years, supporting later de-intensification. Guideline narrative

POUT follow-up (trial practice, not guideline)

Imaging at 3, 6, 9 (chest), 12, 18, 24, 30 (chest), and 36 months, then annually to 60 months; cystoscopy every 6 months to 24 months, then annually to 60 months. Use EAU for routine clinic schedules; POUT shows how intensively the landmark DFS data were collected.

AUA/SUO 2023 addresses surveillance/survivorship in non-metastatic UTUC (Coleman J Urol; PMID 37096584); detailed interval tables were not retrieved beyond the abstract—prefer EAU intervals above for day-to-day scheduling.

How to read the “curve” clinically

Time after RNU (surveillance arm, mixed high-risk POUT) 0 ── early drop (median DFS ~30 mo) ── 3y ~46% DFS ── 5y ~45% DFS (plateau-ish) Most systemic risk: years 0–2 (esp. lung) Bladder risk: ongoing early years; guideline notes decline after ~4 y
  • Treatment effect consistent across pathologic stage (including pT2) in forest plots—do not invent a separate pT2N0 % DFS.
  • Multivariable: higher stage worse (pT3 vs pT2 HR ~1.6; pT4 vs pT2 HR ~2.8 for DFS in primary appendix)—so pT2N0 is the better end of the POUT population, but still high-risk enough for adjuvant consideration.
  • Observational high-risk series (Martini 2022 Eur Urol Oncol, PMID 35504834): under EAU-style follow-up, non-bladder recurrence risk at 2 years still substantial (~42% without prior bladder cancer in that report)—supports keeping imaging dense through year 2.
  • Bladder vs systemic: In POUT, NMIBC bladder events were not DFS events (MIBC bladder was censoring/sensitivity). Counsel bladder surveillance separately from metastatic DFS numbers.

5. Management pathway

  1. Confirm complete RNU, pT2N0, M0, margins, GFR, recovery.
  2. ≤90 days, platinum-fit → offer 4 cycles gemcitabine–cisplatin (GFR ≥50) or gemcitabine–carboplatin (GFR 30–49) per POUT.
  3. Declines / unfit platinum → surveillance ± discuss adjuvant nivolumab only with all-MIUC (CheckMate 274) boundary—no verified UTUC-only benefit to quote.
  4. Intravesical single-shot chemo if indicated (no prior bladder cancer)—bladder-recurrence prevention.
  5. Then EAU high-risk surveillance as above.
  6. Recurrence → local/bladder: urologic management; metastatic → mUC systemic pathway.

7. Patient counseling: regimens discussed

Adjuvant gemcitabine–platinum (POUT)

  • What it is: Four 21-day cycles after RNU for muscle-invasive/node-positive UTUC.
  • Schedule / dosing: Gemcitabine 1000 mg/m² d1,8 + cisplatin 70 mg/m² d1 (GFR ≥50) or carboplatin AUC 4.5–5 if GFR 30–49; start within 90 days.
  • How given: IV outpatient; CBC/renal labs each cycle.
  • Expected benefits: About 71 in 100 vs 46 in 100 disease-free at 3 years; 62% vs 45% at 5 years. OS trend (66% vs 57% at 5 y) is supportive but adjusted OS not definitive.
  • Common / serious risks: Grade ≥3 events ~44% (neutropenia 36%, platelets 10%, febrile neutropenia 6%, nausea/vomiting 6%); no treatment-related deaths in POUT.
  • Surveillance after: Stop chemo after 4 cycles; then cystoscopy/cytology and CTU/chest as above.
  • Evidence: Birtle 2020 Lancet PMID 32145825; Birtle 2024 JCO PMID 38350047.

Surveillance alone (POUT control)

  • What it is: Structured imaging + cystoscopy without adjuvant chemo.
  • Expected outcomes: Roughly half have a DFS event by ~3 years in the mixed high-risk trial population (median DFS ~30 months).
  • Evidence: Same POUT papers.

Single postoperative intravesical chemotherapy

  • What it is: One bladder instillation after RNU (selected patients) to lower bladder recurrence—not a substitute for systemic adjuvant therapy.
  • Evidence: AUA/SUO / EAU frameworks (Coleman PMID 37096584; EAU follow-up chapter).
