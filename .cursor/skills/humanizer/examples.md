# Humanizer Examples

## Publication Pass Example

### Input

The findings are really quite important in many different ways, and experts say they could potentially have a very large impact across multiple settings.

### Output

These findings are potentially impactful across multiple care settings, but the magnitude and generalizability require confirmation in external cohorts.

### Why

- replaces vague attribution
- removes filler and hedging clutter
- adds publication-appropriate precision
